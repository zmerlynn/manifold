## 1. THE LOCAL BOUNDARY ANSWER (witness theorem, general form)

Setup. A transversal 3-face triple point p is where three input faces f,g,h have
planes meeting at p, with p in the relative interior of all three overlaps. The
three planes cut a small 3-ball around p into 3-cells (up to 8 for three generic
planes; fewer when two dihedrals coincide). Winding w is constant on each 3-cell.
On face f, the two seams S_fg = plane(f)^plane(g) and S_fh = plane(f)^plane(h) cross
at p; that 2D crossing splits f near p into 4 quadrant sub-faces. Likewise g and h.

Multiplicity. Each quadrant sub-face of f is a piece of the SINGLE input triangle f.
Crossing it (from +n_f to -n_f) changes w by exactly the coincident signed cover on
that sub-face. For three MUTUALLY TRANSVERSAL planes there is no coincidence, so the
cover is f's own single sheet: m = +1. Same for g,h. So EVERY sub-face at a
transversal triple point is mult-1: w_below = w_above + 1, universally (this is s7b's
result, reg3d-s7b, and the L1880 witness-theorem comment).

The boundary. d{w>=1} passes through a sub-face iff exactly one of its two bordering
3-cells is in {w>=1}. For a mult-1 sub-face that is w_above <= 0 && w_above+1 >= 1,
i.e. EXACTLY w_above == 0. This is precisely the current EmitSeamedFace rule
(L1888). The winding VALUES differ per quadrant (each quadrant is on a different
side of g and h, so w_above there picks up g's and h's contributions), but the RULE
is identical and the jump is always +1.

So the local boundary at a transversal triple point is a BOOK of single (mult-1)
sheets: one arrangement quadrant sub-face per (face in {f,g,h}) x (quadrant with
w_above==0), meeting pairwise along the seam segments through p, oriented toward the
-n (solid) side. There are:
  - NO coincident/multi-sheet outputs (a piece of one input triangle yields at most
    one output sheet);
  - NO winding-jump-by-2 across any single face (that requires COPLANAR double
    cover, m=2, which is the fold's job, not a transversal triple point's);
  - correctly EMPTY output on the everted (w_above<0) and buried (w_above>=1)
    strata - both sides same class, no boundary, the RIGHT answer.

"w jumps -2 -> +3 across a seam" is a category error about the data. No single seam
carries a jump of 5. -2 and +3 are winding values of two DIFFERENT 3-cells in the
neighbourhood, reached by crossing SEVERAL faces; each crossing is +-1. The mult-1
witness rule reads each of those +-1 transitions correctly.

Does the pair/fold multiplicity algebra generalize, or is a new case needed? The
fold's m-algebra (FoldCoplanarClusters L2099-2124: m = net in-plane cover, retain
iff aboveIn != belowIn, orient by the solid side, and the self-check wb-wa==m) IS
ALREADY the general emission rule. The seamed path is its m==1 specialization (it
skips wb and uses wb=wa+1). At a transversal triple point m==1 on every sub-face, so
the specialization is exact and NO new case is needed. The only m!=1 case is
coplanar coincidence, which the fold already owns. (See section 8 for the ONE
genuine emission subtlety: a SEAMED face that is ALSO unclustered-coplanar carries
m!=1 and the seamed path's implicit m=1 then mis-emits - a clustering-completeness
gap, not a triple-point gap.)


## 2. THE CLOSURE THEOREM (the load-bearing result)

THEOREM. Let X be a VALID arrangement cell complex of the component: (i) every
pairwise seam crossing is a 0-cell; (ii) every 2-cell is a maximal uncrossed
sub-face of one input triangle (transversal) or one coplanar cluster; (iii) every
2-cell borders exactly two 3-cells; (iv) w is constant on each 3-cell and jumps by
the 2-cell multiplicity m across it. Then emitting each 2-cell oriented toward its
{w>=1} side (iff exactly one bordering 3-cell has w>=1) yields d{w>=1}, which is a
CLOSED oriented 2-manifold - every edge balanced.

PROOF SKETCH. {w>=1} is a well-defined subset of R^3 (w is a single-valued integer
field off the surface). The topological boundary of any subset of R^3 is a closed
set with no boundary of its own (d(dS) = empty); realized on the cell complex X it
is a cycle in the 2-chain sense, so every 1-cell (edge) has equal incoming and
outgoing emitted halfedges. The per-2-cell "exactly one side in {w>=1}" test is
exactly the indicator-difference of {w>=1} across the 2-cell, and summing indicator
differences around any edge telescopes to zero. QED.

COROLLARY (the wall reclassification). An UNBALANCED emission fan (SplitTouching
Sheets B1, an open edge) is IMPOSSIBLE from a valid X. Therefore any open edge PROVES
X is invalid; it can NEVER prove that a new emission rule is missing. For transversal
faces (m=1, fixed) the emission rule is complete by construction of the theorem.

This is the emission-first lever. It converts "is the emission rule incomplete?"
(the F4 lane's framing) into "is the constructed arrangement valid?" - a construction
question. The answer to the first is provably NO.

       Measured = 5. Small (most images agree within eps, so the eps-weld fuses

    M2 COMBINATORIAL: even where the 3 images agree in POSITION, the three faces'

       crossing set, so a sub-face edge on f has no matching partner sub-face on g
       (or a crossing is dropped on one face and kept on another). The tiling is not

  By the theorem the ~116 non-twin open edges are NECESSARILY M2 (a valid X cannot
  open). The F4 lane's twin-scan tested only M1 ("dominantly not weld twins") and

  exactly reg3d-c1a's PokedCube finding ("the fans need MORE faces") and reg3d-wjump's

- 8 radial ties + 1 material overlap (SplitTouchingSheets B3/B4): each SEAM edge is
  2-sheet (reg3d-radial: every corpus arrangement EDGE is exactly two planes; a
  triple point is a 0-cell where three 2-sheet seams meet, NOT a >2-sheet 1-cell).
  With a once-only triple point the radial fan at each seam edge stays 2 fwd + 2 bwd,
  so these ties are per-face-grafting artifacts that collapse under a valid X - OR
  they are genuine sub-eps coplanar coincidences (fold's job). Small residue; see

VERDICT: HOLES ARE NOT INTRINSIC. The 121 open edges are arrangement invalidity
(dominantly M2 combinatorial) from the prototype's per-face grafting. They close

FALSIFIER of MY verdict (honest): if, after a valid once-only + consistently-tiled

## 4. THE DESIGN (emission side; data structures, call sites, LOC by file)

Emission needs to CONSUME a valid arrangement and HARDEN its own soundness. It does
NOT build the triple point (composable - construction lane owns that).

Data structures (src/overlap3.cpp, BuildArrangement L1239, ~12 LOC):
  struct TriplePoint { vec3 pos; };                 // ONE canonical 3D per triple
  std::vector<TriplePoint> triplePts;               // filled by construction lane
  // per incident face, the 2D-projected crossings that map to a triple point id:
  std::vector<std::vector<std::pair<vec2,int>>> faceTP;  // faceTP[f] = {(proj2D, tpId)}
The construction lane populates triplePts (once-only) and faceTP (which triple
points cross on each face, projected into that face's frame). EMISSION treats these
as read-only inputs. The KEY invariant construction must meet is in section 6.

Call site 1 - EmitSeamedFace (L1801-1894), consume the shared vertex (~25 LOC):
  Replace the pos2in "no input preimage -> fail closed" refusal (L1846-1851) with:
  a 2D subdivision vertex with no INPUT preimage is looked up in faceTP[f] (nearest
  projected crossing within eps -> tpId); its 3D image is triplePts[tpId].pos (the
  SHARED once-only vertex), NOT a fresh plane-frame back-projection. So getOrAdd2D
  pushes canon3 = triplePts[tpId].pos. This is the F4 prototype's ~10 lines but
  pointing every incident face's sub-face at ONE 3D vertex -> welds by construction,
  no eps-weld reliance, no M1. A crossing with neither an input preimage nor a
  faceTP entry stays fail-closed (a triple point construction declined to build).

Call site 2 - EmitSeamedFace retention, the soundness backstop (~20 LOC, section 8):
  probe wb = RobustWinding(cen3 - eps*nHat) alongside the existing wa; assert
  wb - wa == +1; if not, FAIL CLOSED (an m!=1 seamed sub-face = unclustered coplanar
  coincidence, must not silently mis-emit). Retention becomes the fold's rule
  aboveIn != belowIn, orient by the solid side - unifying the seamed and fold
  retention into ONE rule (a simplification, and byte-identical on transversal m=1).

No other emission call site changes. FoldCoplanarClusters, EmitCleanFaces,
SplitTouchingSheets, BuildImpl are untouched by the emission design.

LOC by file:

  test/overlap3_test.cpp ~80 LOC (openscad resolve pin flip; GWN per-face membership

Emission's ONLY predicate use is the winding probe: RobustWinding at the sub-face
centroid cen3 +- eps*nHat (a ray cast whose crossing tests are orient3d on input
triangle vertices with a constructed ray origin). The mission asks specifically:
does emission need the exact-on-CONSTRUCTED-points kernel at interior witnesses of
constructed sub-faces?

MEASURED NO. The F4 census reports B5 (RobustWinding returns nullopt = filter-
uncertain) fires ZERO times on openscad, INCLUDING after the completion prototype
runs the winding probe on the constructed triple-point sub-face centroids - the
prototype's wall is purely emission fan-imbalance (121 unbal), not a winding
fail-close. So the winding probe DECIDES robustly at constructed centroids: a
quadrant centroid is generically off every plane, and the ray-cast crossing signs do
not graze. Emission is SUBSTRATE-AGNOSTIC on openscad.

The exact plane-based-rep / vendor-Shewchuk tripwire is therefore NOT an emission
requirement. It is a CONSTRUCTION requirement (the exact radial order / consistent
tiling at a shallow-dihedral triple point), which lives in the construction lane. The
clean composability boundary: emission consumes a valid arrangement over whatever
substrate; it never orients a constructed rational point itself.

ONE contingency (gated behind measurement): if a denser carrier makes the winding
probe graze at a constructed centroid (B5 > 0), emission would need the reg3d-c2bx
WindingAt escalation on a CONSTRUCTED point - and that escalation is "total only on
input doubles" today, so it WOULD hit the tripwire. Not forced by openscad (B5=0).
Mitigation short of the tripwire: re-sample the winding at several interior points of
the sub-face (EmitCleanFaces already does this graze-dodge for clean faces, L2166
kBary; extend the same multi-sample loop to the seamed sub-face centroid). ~10 LOC,
emission-owned, banked until B5>0 is observed.


## 6. COMPOSABILITY - what survives if the triple point comes from another lane

The construction of the triple point (once-only 3D vertex + which crossings tile each
face) is a SEPARATE lane. Everything below survives regardless of HOW it is built
(double cache vs exact plane-based symbolic vertex):

  (a) The closure theorem (section 2) and the local-boundary answer (section 1) -
      pure emission math, construction-independent.
  (b) The seamed m-check soundness backstop (section 8) - no construction dependency;
      it hardens emission against ANY arrangement (valid or not) and fails closed
      rather than mis-emit.
  (c) The winding-probe substrate-agnosticism (section 5, B5=0) - emission needs no
      exact-on-constructed kernel.
  (d) The oracle harness (section 7) - validates any construction's output.

THE INVARIANT emission requires of the construction lane (the contract, the only
thing that matters for the fans to close):
  I1. ONCE-ONLY 3D: the triple point has ONE canonical 3D position, referenced by
      every sub-face of all three incident faces (kills M1).
  I2. CONSISTENT TILING: each seam S is split at the SAME set of 3D crossing points
      on BOTH its incident faces; equivalently, the shared triple-point vertex is
      INSERTED into each incident face's edge list (splitting its seams explicitly)
      rather than left for each face's RemoveOverlaps2D to rediscover independently
      (kills M2).
  I3. 2-SHEET EDGES: no seam edge is promoted to a >2-sheet 1-cell (holds on the
      corpus by reg3d-radial; a triple point is a 0-cell).
Emission does not care whether I1/I2 are achieved by a once-only DOUBLE (the 5-twin
measurement suggests 146/151 triple points already agree within eps, so a once-only
double + explicit shared-vertex insertion may satisfy I1/I2 with NO exact kernel - a
composability note that would REFUTE the "tripwire forced" claim, but it is
construction's to prove) or by an exact symbolic vertex. Emission closes either way.


## 7. PINS + ORACLE (multi-sheet emission correctness)

Primary oracle - GWN per-face membership (the reg3d-1b-optB / wjump oracle, the RIGHT
oracle for multi-sheet emission; volume alone masks compensating errors):
  - Independent Van Oosterom-Strackee GWN over the component's RAW soup (not through
    any arrangement or weld). For every EMITTED sub-face, sample GWN at its centroid
    +-eps*n: the retained (-n) side must be genuinely >=1 and the +n side <1 (the
    boundary genuinely separates in from out). For every DROPPED sub-face, both sides
    must be same-class (<1 both, or >=1 both). This is the per-face membership check
    that a multi-sheet / everted emission is correct sheet-by-sheet, not just in
    aggregate. It is what proved GT7863's w=-1 drop correct (1b-optB Step 3) and it
    directly catches the failure mode where a compensating pair of errors balances the
    fans but emits outside {w>=1}.
  - Aggregate: resolvedVol == GWN-MC {w>=1} volume; tol-invariance (0.5eps == eps);
    IsSelfIntersecting false; IsManifold && Is2Manifold; decompose count preserved.
    (GWN-MC volume can be oracle-unusable on GT7081-scale near-flat sheets, 1b-optB;
    fall back to signed-volume == resolvedVol + per-face membership there.)

Pins:
  P1 (resolve): openscad regularizes oracle-graded (GWN per-face membership + volume
     band + tol-invariance + non-self-int). Currently the F4 fail-closed pin.
  P2 (s7b no-op): every transversal single face and every RESOLVING corpus carrier
     (siA/siB/folds/bars/caps/bridged) byte-identical (FNV) to HEAD - the emission
     change is a provable no-op off the triple-point sites (getOrAdd2D never adds; the
     m-check passes with jump==+1; retention byte-identical when wb=wa+1).
  P3 (m-check load-bearing): a synthetic seamed-AND-unclustered-coplanar carrier
     mis-emits at HEAD (silent) and FAILS CLOSED with the m-check; disabling the check
     reverts to the silent mis-emit (mutation red).
  P4 (shared-vertex load-bearing): reverting getOrAdd2D to the per-face back-projection
     re-opens openscad's fans (mutation red) - anchors that consistency, not a rule
     change, is what closes it.
  P5 (holes verdict): the decisive measurement (section 9) - open-edge count drops to
     ~0 under a valid construction with the emission rule unchanged.


## 8. THE ONE GENUINE EMISSION SUBTLETY (the m-check, and what it is NOT)

The seamed path (EmitSeamedFace) probes only wa and assumes wb = wa+1 (m=1). This is
correct for a transversal single face. It is WRONG for a seamed face that is ALSO
coplanar-coincident with an UNCLUSTERED neighbour (m=2): then wb = wa+2 and the
mult-1 rule ("emit iff wa==0") drops a wa==-1 face that is genuinely on d{w>=1}. This
is the reg3d-7863c1 mechanism (DetectCoplanarClusters skipping shares-vertex pairs
left a flat face's tiles unclustered), one path over: it can reach the SEAMED path,
not the fold. openscad is coplanar-DOMINATED shallow-dihedral, the worst case for it.

This is the ONLY place the emission RULE (not the arrangement) needs generalizing,
and it is the failure the F4 lane MIS-ATTRIBUTED to triple points. The fix is the
m-check backstop (section 4 call site 2): compute wb, require wb-wa == +1 for the
seamed path, else fail closed. The proper CLOSURE of the fail-closed is clustering
completeness (extend reg3d-7863c1's two-pass to admit seamed-face coplanar tiles),
not a triple-point rule. Whether it fires on openscad is a measurement (does the
m-check trip on any openscad seamed sub-face?). If yes, route those faces through the
fold's m-algebra; if no, the backstop is a pure never-fires soundness rail.

## 10. TOP 3 FAILURE MODES (adversarial)

FM1 - WINDING GRAZE AT A CONSTRUCTED CENTROID (predicate-fork contingency,
  fail-closed or tripwire). openscad is already triple-point-dense; a near-QUADRUPLE
  point (a fourth plane grazing a triple-point sub-face centroid) makes the winding
  ray-cast graze on a CONSTRUCTED origin, outside the exact kernel's input-double
  domain -> B5 fail-closed at best, tripwire at worst. Measured B5=0 today, but this
  is the closest live edge. Mitigation: multi-sample the sub-face interior (extend
  EmitCleanFaces' kBary graze-dodge, ~10 LOC) before any escalation. This is
  emission-owned and bounded; only a genuine constructed-point tie forces the tripwire.

FM2 - WINDING-READ INCONSISTENCY ACROSS THE THREE FACES (silent-wrong, the deepest
  risk). Even with a valid TILING, if the winding probe reads different w on two
  sub-faces that border the SAME 3-cell (their centroids straddle a fourth grazing
  face, or two ray-casts disagree), emission retains one and drops the other -> an
  open edge that is NOT tiling-invalidity, or worse a compensating pair that balances
  the fans but emits a face outside {w>=1} (a CLOSED but WRONG output). The m-check
  and the re-gate (IsSelfIntersecting + Is2Manifold) are partial backstops but a
  compensating error can pass both. ONLY the GWN per-face membership oracle (section
  7) catches it. This is why the oracle is per-face membership, not aggregate volume.

FM3 - m!=1 SEAMED SUB-FACE FROM UNCLUSTERED COPLANAR COINCIDENCE (section 8). The
  genuine emission subtlety, and openscad's coplanar-dominated geometry is its worst
  case. Without the m-check the current seamed path silently drops wa==-1 double-cover
  boundary faces (the exact face the F4 lane thought needed a "winding-jump>=2 rule").
  With the m-check it fails closed; the proper fix is clustering completeness. If the
  m-check fires broadly on openscad, the emission design must route seamed coplanar
  tiles through the fold's m-algebra (a larger change than +57 LOC) - so the m-check
  firing rate on openscad is a gating measurement alongside P5.
