## 1. OrientProj2D is a duplicate of ExactOrient2DDrop  [PROVEN bitwise]

Site: src/overlap3.cpp:2130-2138 (OrientProj2D def) + its 3 callers in
InputVertexStrictlyOnEdge (2141-2144).

OrientProj2D and ExactOrient2DDrop (1958) are the SAME predicate FORM: a 2D orient
of three points evaluated as a padded orient3d (embed at z=0, lift the FIRST point
in +z), filter-then-exact through the ONE blessed Orient3DFilterSign ->
Orient3DExactSign chain. OrientProj2D takes explicit 2D coords; ExactOrient2DDrop
takes three vec3 + an axis to drop. InputVertexStrictlyOnEdge's collinearity test
is the three axis-drops:
  OrientProj2D(V.x,V.y,...) == ExactOrient2DDrop(V,a,b,2)  (drop z)
  OrientProj2D(V.y,V.z,...) == ExactOrient2DDrop(V,a,b,0)  (drop x)
  OrientProj2D(V.z,V.x,...) == ExactOrient2DDrop(V,a,b,1)  (drop y; coord order
      transposed -> sign FLIP, but the decision is `== 0`, flip-invariant)

Simplification: replace the 3 OrientProj2D calls with ExactOrient2DDrop, DELETE
OrientProj2D (~7 net lines). Folds the last duplicate 2D-orient wrapper onto the
shared exact scaffold - directly answers the brief's "ExactSegProperCross/
ExactOrient2DDrop vs the exact-escalation helpers - shared scaffolding?": they
already share the FORM; this was the one remaining redundant wrapper.

expected-bitwise? YES. Same exact FORM; the collinear decision is all-three-==0,
invariant to the per-axis sign flip. The arm only fires on openscad (input-vertex-
on-edge is a no-op off openscad), so resolving carriers are untouched by
construction.
risk: LOW. Confined to the fail-closed input-vertex T-junction arm.
COHERENCE follow-ups (do with the fold): drop "OrientProj2D/" from the kernel-

## EnumerateTriplePoints vs BuildJunctionRegistry vs the input-vertex arm

They do NOT subsume one another - they are a 3-LAYER pipeline, each producing a
distinct vertex KIND:
- EnumerateTriplePoints (:2000) CONSTRUCTS the 3-plane triple points (the only
  Intersect3Planes constructor) AND pre-splits the two crossing seams at the exact
  2D crossing (A.seamTriples).
- BuildJunctionRegistry (:2229) UNIONS every once-only vertex - seam endpoints +
  triples (read out of seamTriples) + input-vertex T-junctions - into ONE flat
  eps-deduped list (A.junctions), so ANY emitted edge on ANY face splits at any
  interior registry vertex.
- The input-vertex arm (CollectInputVertexTJunctions, :2170) ADDS input-vertex-
  on-edge T-junctions to that union.
Genuinely complementary (construct -> register -> consume). The TEMPTING unify -
drop the seamTriples pre-split channel and let A.junctions carry the proper-
crossing split too (triples are already pushed into the registry, :2241-2243) - is
NOT safe: the seamTriples path uses the EXACT SegLineIntersect2D 2D crossing and
has NO strict-parameter guard, whereas JunctionSplitsOnSegment applies the
tlo/thi = eps/len interior guard, so it would EXCLUDE triples near a seam endpoint
that seamTriples catches. On openscad's near-tangent geometry these differ; off
openscad both are empty (no triples), so it is untestable on resolving carriers and
risky on the one carrier it touches. VERDICT: do not merge; they are distinct.
The only real code-sharing win in this cluster is finding #1 (OrientProj2D).

## ExactSegProperCross / ExactOrient2DDrop vs the WindCrossTri chain

Already well-shared: both route through the ONE blessed Orient3DFilterSign ->
Orient3DExactSign FORM. ExactOrient2DDrop is the 2D-orient wrapper (padded
orient3d); WindCrossTri is the 3D straddle+interior chain. Different geometric
tests on the same kernel - correctly kept distinct. cleanPierce already folded
onto WindCrossTri (simp3, landed). OrientProj2D was the one leftover duplicate
(finding #1). No further merge is sound.

## Winding BVH + seed-sign precompute + two-pass parallel dispatch (perf arc)

Not dead weight. The BVH sub-stack (BuildTriWindBVH / WindCandidates /
WindingAtCands / PrecomputeSeedSign / RobustWindingBVH) is single-purpose for
EmitCleanFaces' hot loop, each with exactly one caller in a clean layer. The
seamed/fold paths deliberately stay on the O(nTri) walk (RobustWinding) - "a BVH is
the later pass" - so it is an INCOMPLETE perf rollout, not redundancy. The two-pass
parallel dispatch driver already had its single-component `else` twin removed
(simp3 #2, landed). MINOR observation (not recommended): CollectInputVertexTJunctions
builds its OWN TriWindBVH (:2178) separate from EmitCleanFaces' (:2843) - two
per-component builds; shareable but the build is per-component-cheap and threading
one through BuildJunctionRegistry -> EmitComponentBoundary costs more plumbing than
it saves. Flag only.
