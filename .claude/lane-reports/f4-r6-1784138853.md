## THE MECHANISM, PINNED BY READING (the crux for R6)

The cross-face inconsistency the hypothesis targets is the PER-FACE 2D eps-merge
INSIDE RemoveOverlaps2D. The 3D inputs are already shared (seam endpoints,
triples), but each face PROJECTS to its own 2D frame and merges within eps THERE.
Orthonormal projection is NON-EXPANDING (drops the normal component), so two 3D
points at distance d project to 2D distance <= d. A NEAR-TANGENT face compresses
the 3D separation into the normal direction, so it can collapse two 3D-distinct
points (up to ~1e-4 apart, per r4/r5) to within eps in 2D and merge them, while a
less-tangent face keeps them 2D-distinct. THAT is "different faces merge
differently".

CONSEQUENCE for the quotient: to make this merge consistent across faces, the
global 3D quotient would have to MERGE the very points a near-tangent face
collapses - i.e. 3D points up to ~1e-4 apart (r4/r5: thin-cell triple points
spread 1e-4 down to ~2*eps). Merging 3D points 1e-4 apart is a ~1e-4 displacement,
>> eps (~3.5e-10). That is precisely Route-B's refuted move (blows the eps guard
by 4-7 orders, fires on GT7081/GT7863's legitimate near-tangent walls), just
relocated from wall planes to seam-graph vertices. A within-eps quotient (the
only guarded, in-form option) merges only the ~2*eps clusters and leaves the
near-tangent 2D collapses per-face-inconsistent -> predicts NO change to the 22.

PLAN: build the in-form within-eps transitive quotient anyway and MEASURE the
trajectory (the mission requires measurement, not reasoning); characterize
exactly; then price the terminal.
