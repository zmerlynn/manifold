## SOURCE READ - the two emission retention rules (the load-bearing asymmetry)

EmitSeamedFace (overlap3.cpp L2069, the TRANSVERSAL seamed path):
  - probes ONE side: g = RobustWinding(cen3 + eps*nHat)  [w_above only]
  - retains iff *g == 0; assumes m=1 (w_below = w_above + 1)
  - classify point = LargestSubTri CENTROID (no incenter, no jitter)
  - emits ORIGINAL orientation always (solid assumed on -nHat side)

FoldCoplanarClusters (overlap3.cpp L2237, the COPLANAR path):
  - probes BOTH sides: wa = RobustWinding(cen3+eps*nHat), wb = RobustWinding(cen3-eps*nHat)
  - computes m = net in-plane signed cover; self-checks wb-wa == m (else fail closed)
  - retains iff aboveIn != belowIn (aboveIn = wa>=1, belowIn = wb>=1)
  - orients by solid side (belowIn -> original, aboveIn -> reversed)
  - classify point = largest sub-tri INCENTER + generic jitter (0.25*inrad in dir
    (0.4359,0.9000)) to dodge axis-aligned member-plane grazes
