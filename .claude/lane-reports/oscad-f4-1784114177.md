## CLOSURE VERDICT: B3 does NOT close in-form (mission item 4: PRICE, do not build)

The completion DISSOLVES F4 but does NOT make openscad resolve - it reveals the
already-priced C-1a plane-based-rep wall, and its per-face triple-point construction
is not globally sound. Two coupled residuals, both the plane-based substrate:

RESIDUAL-1  ONCE-ONLY TRIPLE-POINT CONSTRUCTION (~151 distinct triple points).
  A triple point {f,g,h} is reconstructed 3x today (once per incident face's plane
  frame) -> 3 double-rounded images. For the emitted sub-faces to weld, all 3 must be
  bit-identical (once-only), else the near-parallel tail cracks (R1 - 5 twins already
  survive even the small case; openscad is coplanar-dominated shallow-dihedral, the
  worst tail). Beyond the point POSITION, the RADIAL structure around the triple point
  (which sub-faces meet, in what order) must agree across the 3 faces' independent 2D
  arrangements - the arrangement-vertex consistency the per-face driver cannot supply.
  This is the C-5 "one canonical construction shared across phases" = plane-based rep.

RESIDUAL-2  C-1a / C-1b OPEN-BOUNDARY EMISSION (121 open edges).
  Even with once-only triple points, the per-face mult-1 emission (retain iff
  w_above==0) DROPS faces on the everted double-sheet (w_above in [-2,-1] -> both sides
  exterior) and the high-cover strata (w_above up to +3), leaving 121 holes. Needs the
  winding-jump>=2 double-sheet re-emission (s7b refuted the simple orientation flip) +
  exact arrangement at the degenerate corners = the SAME C-1a escalation GT7081/GT7863
  sit behind.
