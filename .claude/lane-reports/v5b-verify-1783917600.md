- The doc does NOT literally contradict itself (line 179 scopes B2; line 367 flags
  GT7863 intrinsic). But the "no exact-zeros / input orient3d FP-recoverable with

  Critically, GT7863's exact-zeros are handled by the SHADOWS ONE-CONVENTION

  two mechanisms (margin for near-tangency, convention for exact ties) both live
