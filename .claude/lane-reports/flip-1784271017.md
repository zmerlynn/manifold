### THE FIELD DESIGN (derived this session; the load-bearing correction)

The field is E(c) = exact winding of the EPSILON-LAYER immediately above the
cell's group plane (+nHat side), NOT the probe-level wA.  Reasons:
- The eps-layer deltas are purely combinatorial: crossing a sub-edge crosses
  exactly the chord-partner sheets ON that sub-edge (a sheet not chording there
  cannot separate the two eps-layers - the act3-cleanflood cleanliness argument,
  airtight as eps->0).  delta = sum over distinct chord partner faces f2 of
  -sgn(dot(t_out, n_f2)), t_out = sigmaF*cross(d3, Nrep), sigmaF the Drop2 frame
  parity ((axis==1?-1:+1)*sign(Nrep[axis])).  No stack-membership question at
  the eps level (a wA-level field was derived and REJECTED: sheets can dip into
  the probe window mid-cell without leaving arrangement segments, making
  wA-level lateral deltas unsound).
- wA converts per-cell VERTICALLY: wA = E - sPosFull where sPosFull = sum of
  sgn(dot(n_f2,nHat)) over covering foreign sheets with 0 < tPos < 2T at cenP
  (computed from nearD, the full list - NOT just the stack, closing the
  (0.99eps, 2T) absorption gap).  The vertical segment at cenP is gap-finder
  certified, so this crossing set is exact at cenP.
- eps-below conversion (for handoff + seed): w(eps-below) = E + ownJump + sZero
  (sZero = covering foreign sheets with tPos == 0 exactly; pathological).
- CROSS-GROUP HANDOFF (the dihedral rule, eps-form = the act3-cleanflood proof
  verbatim): at a member mesh-edge sub-edge with twin face in another group,
  w_imm-own-above(f) is equal on both sides.  w_imm-own-above(f) = E(c) if
  fsgn(f)=+1 else E(c) + ownJump + sZero.  Sub-edges matched across groups by
  (input vid pair, endpoint position bits); skip if any chord rides the same
  sub-edge (not clean).  rho-cluster rep divergence across groups -> unmatched
  (conservative, no edge).
- SEED (hull anchor, generalized from act3-cleanflood): v* = lex-max input
  vertex; p = v* + (d, d^2, d^3) is exterior (w=0) since the lex-max of a
  compact polyhedron is a vertex; p is on the lexsign(n) side of EVERY plane
  through v* (lexsign = sign of first nonzero normal component - pure
  combinatorics, no ray, no graze possible).  For a group with EXACTLY ONE node
  cell touching v*: E = 0 if lexsign(Nrep)=+1 else -(ownJump+sZero).  Ambiguous
  corners (>1 cell = chords through v*): skip that group's seed (residual:
  point-contact sheets through v* could evade the count - accepted,
  differential-guarded).
- ANCHORS: seeds + every probe-certified cell (E = probeWA + sPosFull).  BFS
  with value-conflict detection: ANY conflict (including flood-vs-certified) =
  the DIFFERENTIAL failing -> flood aborts -> today's fatal (fail-closed never
  weakened).  E1_FLOODDIFF=1 forces the solve+differential shadow (byte-clean)
  for corpus-wide validation.
- v1 ROUTING (this session): probes stay primary; the flood fills ONLY
  probe-failed (pending) cells: wA = E - sPosFull, wB = wA + jump, then the
  standard boundary decision + emission from the stored payload.  Unreached
  pending -> the original fatal.  NODES exclude owned/noOwn/dust cells
  (conservative conduits).
