## Key synthesis decisions (how I resolved tensions across lanes)

1. THE FORK IS ABOUT ONE LAYER. sd-self frames exact-or-refuse vs the trade;
   act3-constructA frames kernel-free winding+identity gated on E1. I unified: every
   layer (a)-(g) is Smith / approximate; only (h)=E1 is fork-dependent. Both
   end-states share Stages 0-3; Stage 4 (order-relaxation) is zero-exact-only;
   Stage 5 is the (h) decision. This matches the task's requested stage order.

2. WINDING IS DECOUPLED (sd-self section 3) but the FLOOD is COUPLED to closure
   (act3-constructA Suspect 2). Not a contradiction: the winding VALUE is decoupled
   from the arrangement SIGN decisions (a wrong sign can't change an integer w); the
   flood's DOMAIN (path-independence) requires a closed complex. I stated both:
   delta sign safe, flood well-definedness gated on E1 completion.

3. CLEAN-FACE vs SEAM-GRAPH (act3-toy's correction of orient-attack). orient-attack
   attributed GT7081's winding fires to WindingAt/seam; act3-toy showed 99.4% are
   CLEAN-face and the seam graph can't reach them without cross-face dihedral
   connectivity (research-grade, unbuilt). So I split the winding retirement into
   Stage 1 (clean-face flood, needs the unbuilt connectivity) and Stage 2 (retire
   ray-cast once flood covers both). This is the honest ordering; a single "swap
   winding" stage would have hidden the unbuilt piece.

4. IDENTITY IS FREE BUT NOT FREE-STANDING (nomerge). Level-0 identity is proven
   free, but the equality weld DISCONNECTS if applied before once-only (RouteA
   22->726). So Stage 3 (eps-merge retirement) is GATED on Stage 0 (once-only). I
   made the ordering dependency an explicit abort criterion, not a footnote.

5. E1 PREDICATE IS LANDED, RESIDUE IS NOT (e1-close/e1-verify). The one-predicate
   floor's PREDICATE is already paid at HEAD; what remains is the ARRANGEMENT
   plumbing (symbolic extent + exact 2D arrangement) + W2. I marked (b) as LANDING
   and (h) as predicate-landed-but-completion-open, so the memo doesn't overclaim.

6. NO COUNTS AS FACTS (memory: no-counts-in-docs). Rewrote every measured number as
   a magnitude with a notebook citation ("up to ~a third of the time (act3-toy)",
   "zero disagreements over ~1.3M (homog-design)"). Kept the fire-census caller
   arithmetic (3->1->0) because it is a structural claim, attributed to
   orient-attack.
