## Step 1-3: seed verified + candidate A probed (DECISIVE)

### Seed confirmed (boolean3 coupling mechanism)
boolean3 Winding03_ (src/boolean3.cpp:387-459) computes winding NOT per-vertex
independently: it unites verts across every edge that does NOT cross the other
operand (DisjointSets uA), ray-casts ONE representative per connected
component (Kernel02, +z signed crossings), then FLOOD-FILLS the component. Two
verts in one component cannot disagree - identical by construction. That is the
connectivity coupling. Assembly (boolean_result.cpp PairUp:285-290) then relies
on winding-parity giving clean start-end-start-end alternation along each edge -
GUARANTEED for valid closed operands, and exactly the "unbalanced fan" (1F/0B,
1F/2B) that breaks for self-intersecting soup. Shadows (shared.h:121) is the
one-convention tie-break for EXACT FP equalities (p==q -> dir<0), used
pervasively. So the oracle is a double program: no exact arithmetic anywhere.

READING: candidate A (pure reuse, zero new mechanism) resolves EVERY carrier
that decomposes into non-self-intersecting components - which is all of them
except (a) self_intersectA/B (one self-intersecting shell, nothing to fold) and
(b) openscad (needs orientation-aware Add/Subtract per comp, R5). It even covers

KEY: the "intrinsic" degeneracies (GT7863 vertex-on-face) do NOT break
boolean3's FP+Shadows path. PHILOSOPHICAL COST: the pairs/multi-shells decompose
because they were BUILT by composition/offset of valid parts; a genuinely
self-intersecting single shell (siA/siB) does NOT decompose. So A is a PRODUCT
win (delegate to the machinery we were researching to replace) and a research
NON-ANSWER for true single-shell self-overlap. That residual is B's job.
