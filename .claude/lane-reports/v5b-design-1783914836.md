## Step 1-3: seed verified + candidate A probed (DECISIVE)

### Seed confirmed (boolean3 coupling mechanism)
boolean3 Winding03_ (src/boolean3.cpp:387-459) computes winding NOT per-vertex
independently: it unites verts across every edge that does NOT cross the other
operand (DisjointSets uA), ray-casts ONE representative per connected
component (Kernel02, +z signed crossings), then FLOOD-FILLS the component. Two
verts in one component cannot disagree - identical by construction. That is the
connectivity coupling. Assembly (boolean_result.cpp PairUp:285-290) then relies
on winding-parity giving clean start-end-start-end alternation along each edge -
GUARANTEED for valid closed operands, and exactly the "unbalanced fan" (1F/0B,
1F/2B) that breaks for self-intersecting soup. Shadows (shared.h:121) is the
one-convention tie-break for EXACT FP equalities (p==q -> dir<0), used
pervasively. So the oracle is a double program: no exact arithmetic anywhere.

READING: candidate A (pure reuse, zero new mechanism) resolves EVERY carrier
that decomposes into non-self-intersecting components - which is all of them
except (a) self_intersectA/B (one self-intersecting shell, nothing to fold) and
(b) openscad (needs orientation-aware Add/Subtract per comp, R5). It even covers

KEY: the "intrinsic" degeneracies (GT7863 vertex-on-face) do NOT break

because they were BUILT by composition/offset of valid parts; a genuinely
self-intersecting single shell (siA/siB) does NOT decompose. So A is a PRODUCT
win (delegate to the machinery we were researching to replace) and a research
NON-ANSWER for true single-shell self-overlap. That residual is B's job.

### B crux (2): what transfers vs what breaks (boolean3 source-level)
TRANSFERS unchanged (geometry-only, FP-safe per B2/B3):
  - Shadow01/Kernel02/Kernel11/Kernel12 predicates + Shadows one-convention
    tie-break (shared.h:121) - take vertex positions+normals, operand-agnostic.
  - Winding03 connectivity coupling: DisjointSets unite-across-non-crossing-edge
    + one ray-cast per component + flood fill (boolean3.cpp:400-456). The
    deltas are +-1 INTEGERS (face orientation, robust axis), not near-zero FP.
NEEDS SELF-ADAPTATION (bounded, not a wall):
  - Intersect12 collider enumerates P-edge vs Q-face; for S-vs-S it returns each
    edge's OWN incident faces -> must skip self-adjacent (vertex-sharing) faces.
  - Winding-of-own-vertex is 100% boundary (every S-vert lies on S). Shadows
    expandP perturbation handles it (O(1) each); boolean3 does the SPARSE
    version today - GT7863's cross-operand vertex-exactly-on-face is the
    existence proof (candidate A resolved it winding-exact).
BREAKS (the keystone, a MECHANISM gap not precision):
  - Assembly PairUp (boolean_result.cpp:285-301) pairs edge crossings by 1D

    not go start-end-start-end... this algorithm becomes a heuristic." Valid for

    (triple points). Generic 2-sheet transversal self-intersection reduces to the
    2-operand case; the >2-sheet junctions are the open gap = exactly the June
    memo's standing "serial-seam-matcher / triple-point rule" kill and the v5
    doc's "radial-order DCEL remains" finding. NOT reuse; new mechanism.

### B verdict: NEEDS-CHANGE (live, iterate) - precision is NOT the barrier
The v5 "barrier is precision" refines to "precision GIVEN INDEPENDENT
decisions." Existence proof: boolean3 resolves all pairs in doubles via coupling
(candidate A confirmed, incl. intrinsic GT7863). For self-overlap, MEASURED: the
winding decision is FP-safe (B1/B3) and input orient3d is FP-safe (B2). The
residual barriers are (i) UNMEASURED level-2 predicates on CONSTRUCTED
intersection points (the v5 doc's real precision concern; shared with the exact
design - both construct points); (ii) the assembly's radial ordering at
multi-sheet edges (MECHANISM). So B is LIVE on the winding+input axis; kernel
avoidance is plausible for the winding half and the common-case arrangement; the
open work is the assembly and the constructed-point predicates - the next round.
