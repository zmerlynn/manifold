## S3 - RADIAL-ASSEMBLY GAP SIZING

Method. For the composed Havoc soup (left ++ right, 176 tri, fully exact) and the
genuine single-shell residuals self_intersectA / self_intersectB (17160 tri each,
grid broadphase + exact narrow phase), enumerate every pairwise face-face
intersection LINE, group faces per exact supporting line, and count DISTINCT
PLANES per line (coplanar faces grouped first). A ">2 distinct plane" line is a
genuine multi-sheet / triple-point edge - the structure that needs radial ordering
and that PairUp cannot serve. At each such line measure the pairwise radial gaps
as the normalized triple product sign(dot(e, cross(r_i,r_j))) on input face
normals (e = edge direction) - exactly |sin(theta)| between the incident
half-sheets, the deciding predicate's margin.

Cross-check: my exact broadphase reproduced the pipeline's own seam counts
bit-for-bit on all three carriers (Havoc 89, siA 338, siB 338 - the FindSeams
counts from the wall-A anatomy), so the enumeration is complete, not sampled.

### Finding 1 - ZERO genuine triple points in the residual carriers.

  carrier   intersection lines   distinct-plane histogram   genuine multi-sheet (>2 planes)
  -------   ------------------   ------------------------   -------------------------------
  Havoc     297                  {2: 297}                   0
  siA       26078                {2: 26078}                 0
  siB       26078                {2: 26078}                 0

Every arrangement edge in all three carriers is a TWO-DISTINCT-PLANE edge = PairUp's
two-operand shape (4 half-sheets: two faces, two sides each). Not one edge carries
more than two distinct planes. The 26078 siA/siB lines decompose as 25740 mesh-edge
dihedrals (= 3*17160/2, confirming a closed 2-manifold combinatorially) + 338 clean
2-sheet self-intersection crossings. Havoc's 297 = 225 non-coplanar dihedrals + the
rest, with 89 of them being left-vs-right self-crossings.

### Finding 2 - the "multi-sheet by FACE count" lines are coplanar TESSELLATION.

Havoc has 14 lines with >2 FACES (11 triple, 3 quad by face count). Every one is
still exactly 2 distinct PLANES: the extra faces are coplanar tessellation triangles
of one sheet (e.g. line L0 = {f165,f169 | f63,f67}, two coplanar pairs crossing).
The exactly-coincident (gap=0) half-sheet adjacencies I measured (34 on Havoc) are
ALL these tessellation seams - same sheet, adjacent triangles - which the coplanar
grouping (arr.face2Group) folds into one sheet before any radial decision. Zero of
them are inter-sheet coplanar coincidences. siA/siB have ZERO exactly-coplanar face
pairs at all (organic mesh, every triangle a distinct plane).

So the coplanar family interacts cleanly: gap=0 means "same sheet, group it," a
decided operation, never an undecidable radial tie.

### Finding 3 - the radial / dihedral margins are enormous, nowhere near a tie.

  carrier   self-intersections   min |sin(dihedral)|   median   sub-errbound(1e-13)   near-tangent(<1e-6)
  -------   ------------------   -------------------   ------   -------------------   -------------------
  Havoc     89                   3.206e-07             0.959    0                     6 (all vs right f173)
  siA       338                  1.305e-01             0.769    0                     0
  siB       338                  1.305e-01             0.769    0                     0

siA/siB: the closest self-crossing is sin=0.13 (~7.5 deg) - every self-intersection

grazing the left surface = the ~50 eps edge-edge surface contact the probe found),
still ~6 orders above the errbound. NONE sub-errbound on any carrier.

### S3 reconciliation with the wall-A "bitwise-zero angular tie" finding.

The wall-A fan anatomy (walla-arc, step 3) found minTieGap/kTie = 0 EXACTLY on the
failing fans. Those gap=0 ties are between EMISSION-MANUFACTURED twin cap images:

### S3 VERDICT: gaps are input-predicate-decidable; the triple-point gap does not
materialize on the residual.

On every failing carrier the exact arrangement has NO genuine multi-sheet edge; the
radial decision reduces to PairUp's two-sheet case at every real edge, and the one
predicate that could tie (coplanar coincidence, gap=0) resolves to "group as one
sheet." The dihedral margins are 1e-1 (self shells) to 1e-7 (Havoc near-tangency),
all far above the double error bound. NO counterexample found.

Honest scope of the verdict (adversarial residue, not swept):
- It is a measurement of THREE carriers (the corpus residual), not a proof that no
  self-intersecting shell can carry a genuine triple point. A shell built with 3
  faces exactly co-axial WOULD; the corpus residual does not. The kill's PREMISE
  (dense >2-sheet junctions in self-intersecting shells, BR3) is refuted on the
  corpus, not in general.
- The measurement excludes shared-vertex (fan-adjacent) face pairs. A self-
  intersection passing exactly through a mesh vertex is a multi-sheet 0-CELL, a
  distinct (harder) structure than a 1-cell; the seam-seam crossings the anatomy
  counted (124k on siA) are transversal curve crossings = 0-cells where clean
  2-sheet edges MEET, not shared-line >2-sheet edges (confirmed: 0 lines share a
  supporting line). So the density is many 2-sheet edges around near-coincident
  vertices - the S1/S2/budget axis - not radial multi-sheet-per-edge.
- The anatomy's "dense near-coplanar section" on siA is a SWEEP-PROJECTION
  phenomenon (338 seams -> O(338^2) pairwise x-crossings piling into a narrow
  x-band -> ArrangementBudget), NOT a radial near-tangency. B does no plane sweep,
  so that specific density does not gate B's assembly; it re-appears only as many
  (decidable) 2-sheet edges.


## S4 - SELF-ADAPTED ENUMERATION (the spec)

The soup-vs-soup version of boolean3's pipeline, run on a flagged dirty core. Each
part states what transfers from boolean3, the self-adaptation, and the predicate.

(a) INTERSECTION ENUMERATION (collider - operand-agnostic, VERIFIED).
    boolean3's AABB collider (b.collider_) and Intersect12 read face AABBs and
    positions; nothing is P-vs-Q specific. Self-adaptation: on S-vs-S the collider
    returns each edge's OWN incident (vertex-sharing) faces; SKIP shared-vertex
    pairs and keep only genuine non-adjacent crossings. VERIFIED empirically: a grid
    broadphase with exactly this skip reproduced the pipeline's seam counts on all
    three carriers (89 / 338 / 338). No new mechanism; one filter.

(b) WINDING-DELTA RULE at each crossing. Two-operand boolean3 probes w(P) against Q
    (Winding03). Self-case: every crossing is soup-vs-soup. Delta assignment from
    face orientation ALONE: crossing an oriented face f along path direction t
    changes w by sign(dot(t, n_f)) - an INTEGER, n_f an input normal, robust
    (dihedral margins from S3: >= sin 0.13 on the shells, >= 3.2e-7 on Havoc).
    The witness theorem's w_S is an ABSOLUTE sum over ALL faces; the coupled version
    needs only RELATIVE deltas along a path plus one seed probe per connected
    cell-component.
    INVARIANT that makes path-independence hold: w_S is single-valued off the
    surface iff the oriented soup is a closed 2-cycle (every directed edge paired) -
    then the signed crossing count is a cocycle and the net delta between two cells
    is path-independent. This is exactly the faithfulness gate the probe lanes
    already assert. PREDICATE FAILURES that would violate it: a mis-signed crossing
    (wrong sign(dot(t,n_f)) - excluded by the S3 margins), or a missed/spurious
    crossing (collider incompleteness - excluded by the exact enumeration matching
    the seam count). Around any closed loop the deltas sum to zero exactly; that is
    the coupling's consistency, replacing exactness with structure.

(c) ASSEMBLY - radial pairing at multi-sheet edges, PairUp retired in the dirty
    core. SPEC: around each arrangement edge with axis e, sort the incident
    half-sheets by radial angle using sign(dot(e, cross(r_i, r_j))) on input face
    normals (r_i = e x n_i, the in-plane perpendicular), then pair adjacent
    half-sheets by their winding transition (retain the sector whose w_S >= 1).
    S3 result: on the residual every edge is 2-sheet, so this radial sort has a
    trivial 2-element (4-half-sheet) order = precisely PairUp's two-operand shape;
    PairUp need not actually be retired for these carriers, only generalized in
    principle. HONEST RESIDUAL (named, not swept): PairUp also assumes a clean 1D
    start-end ALTERNATION along the edge; with w_S reaching 2 (the double-covered
    stratum the probe confirmed) the retention multiplicity is >1. AppendPartial
    Edges already emits abs(inclusion) copies, so multiplicity is representable, but
    that the emitted start/end sequence stays a valid alternation under w=2 in the
    self case is NOT tested by S3 (S3 sized triple points, which are absent; the
    alternation-under-multiplicity property is a separate 1D question and is the
    honest next probe for part (c)).

(d) SEED PROBES - one double-precision ray per connected cell-component. Round-1
    measured double w_S = exact at Havoc crux points; S1's sweep hardens this.
    boolean3's Winding03 already does exactly this (unite non-crossing edges,
    ray-cast one representative per component, flood-fill). Self-case: identical,
    on the arrangement's cell-components. The ray degeneracy (grazing a shared
    edge/vertex) is boundary-only (probe lane: 0/3000 interior, 100% on-surface),
    so cell-interior seeds almost never invoke symbolic perturbation.

(e) DIFFERENTIATION FROM THE JUNE SERIAL-SEAM-MATCHER KILL. The June matcher was
    killed because its triple-point rule was UNSPECIFIED (a serial proximity/order
    heuristic with no principled junction behavior) and, per the RSI-#3 kill,
    because independent per-cell raycasts could disagree across a shared edge. The
    S4 spec differs on both axes: (1) the triple-point rule IS specified - the
    radial order is the input predicate sign(dot(e, cross(r_i,r_j))) on input face
    normals, a determinant, not a proximity guess; (2) the winding is COUPLED - one
    seed per component + relative integer deltas propagated across ONCE-ONLY-shared
    constructed points (the S2-lane discipline: each intersection point is
    constructed once and shared by all incident edges, so adjacent cells cannot
    disagree), which is the structural cure for the independent-raycast kill.
    Dependency stated: this requires the shared-construction discipline the S2 lane
    owns - the spec is only as consistent as that once-only-construction guarantee.
    And empirically S3 removes the hardest branch: the residual carriers have no
    triple points, so the specified radial rule is never exercised beyond the
    decidable two-sheet case on the corpus.
