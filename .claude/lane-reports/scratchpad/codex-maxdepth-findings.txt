**Bottom Line**

For the exact algebraic core, the construction grammar is closed at depth 1. The real arrangement objects are built directly from input vertices and face planes: seam endpoints, plane triples, and coplanar edge crossings. The skew “crossings of chords whose endpoints are already constructed” from the lane reports are not genuine grammar objects; they come from eps-injected / rounded phantom structure, especially the junction registry and foot projection paths.

But for `overlap3.cpp` as shipped, there is no single pure “sign of a fixed polynomial in the original input coordinates” theorem for the whole pipeline. The code intentionally feeds rounded constructed doubles and eps decisions back into later steps: `SegPlanePoint`, `Intersect3Planes`, `SegLineIntersect2D`, `JunctionSplitsOnSegment`, `pf.lift`, centroid probes, and the final eps weld. Those are posture/representability decisions, not exact algebraic predicates over the original doubles.

**Max-Degree Theorem**

Using raw input coordinate variables as degree 1:

- Max topological exact predicate in the closed grammar: degree 20.
  This is the general homogeneous `orient2d` of three plane-triple points. Each face-plane triple point has homogeneous coordinates `X,Y,Z` degree 7 and `W` degree 6 after substituting face planes, so a 2D homogeneous determinant term has degree `7 + 7 + 6 = 20`.
- Max shipped production exact topological predicate actually used by `ExactSeamsCross`: degree 8.
  It tests one plane-triple point against triangle edges: two input vertices plus one constructed point, giving degree `max(1+1+6, 1+7) = 8`.
- Max exactized eps-collapse threshold among depth-1 arrangement vertices: degree 26.
  Distance between two plane-triple points has numerator degree `7+6 = 13`; squaring gives degree 26.

The code/document “degree 9” is correct in the implementation’s plane-coefficient generator basis: Cramer coordinates are degree 3 in plane coefficients, and a 3x3 homogeneous `orient2d` is degree 9. In the raw input-coordinate basis requested here, that same primitive expands to degree 20.

**True Depth >1?**

No true exact arrangement decision in the landed grammar requires a point constructed from constructed points. The apparent nested sites are representational or eps-posture artifacts:

- `SegLineIntersect2D` computes a rounded 2D split position from rounded seam endpoints, but the canonical 3D vertex is the once-only plane triple from `Intersect3Planes`: [overlap3.cpp](/tmp/codex-maxdepth/tree/src/overlap3.cpp:2581), [overlap3.cpp](/tmp/codex-maxdepth/tree/src/overlap3.cpp:2599).
- `JunctionSplitsOnSegment` projects a registry vertex to an on-line foot and injects it if it is within eps: [overlap3.cpp](/tmp/codex-maxdepth/tree/src/overlap3.cpp:2834). That is exactly the kind of eps-snapped phantom structure reported by `symwalk`, `exjunct`, and `graze`.
- `EmitSeamedFace` rejects unprepared new 2D crossings from `RemoveOverlaps2D` via the `pos2in` backstop, rather than accepting nested constructed vertices: [overlap3.cpp](/tmp/codex-maxdepth/tree/src/overlap3.cpp:3009).
- Sub-eps collapse is explicitly an eps witness/decline, not a new exact construction: [overlap3.cpp](/tmp/codex-maxdepth/tree/src/overlap3.cpp:2605), [overlap3.cpp](/tmp/codex-maxdepth/tree/src/overlap3.cpp:2620).
