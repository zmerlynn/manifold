1. RECONSTRUCTING BOOLEAN3'S CLOSURE ARGUMENT (from code + thesis ch.4)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The exact local identities (thesis notation; Manifold call sites):

(i) THE SHADOW FUNCTION S_ij (thesis eq 4.1, p.65).  S_ij(oA,oB) is a signed 1D
    "does B shadow A along the (i+j+1)th axis" test, with the tie rule: when the
    projected coordinates are EXACTLY equal, treat B as strictly greater ("an
    arbitrarily small shift in the +axis direction" - the symbolic perturbation).
    Manifold: shared.h:121-123
        inline bool Shadows(double p, double q, double dir) {
          return p == q ? dir < 0 : p < q; }
    and Shadow01 (boolean3.cpp:53-83), whose s01 is a DIFFERENCE of two Shadows
    (boolean3.cpp:63-66) = the level-1 shadow X01.  The tie direction `dir` is
    built from the vertex normals: withSign(expandP, a0xp) - b1exp
    (boolean3.cpp:63, withSign at shared.h:66).

    KEY PROPERTY that everything rides on: `p < q` on two doubles is EXACT and
    TOTAL - it never rounds and has no "uncertain" state.  So a shadow is either a
    certain compare or an EXACT tie broken by `dir`.  There is no third regime.

(ii) THE INTERSECTION FUNCTION X_ij as a SIGNED BOUNDARY SUM (thesis eq after
    4.1).  X_ij(oA,oB) = sum over boundary components c of oA of S_{i-1,j}(c,oB),
    plus sum over boundary components c of oB of S_{i,j-1}(oA,c), with +/- signs.
    Manifold implements exactly this alternating boundary sum:
      - Kernel02 (vertex a0 vs facet b2): s02 = sum over the 3 half-edges of b2 of
        s01, signed by orientation - boolean3.cpp:183
            s02 += s01 * (forward == edgeB[i].isForward ? -1 : 1);
      - Kernel11 (edge p1 vs edge q1): s11 = signed sum of Shadow01 over the 4
        endpoints (2 from p, 2 from q) - boolean3.cpp:109, 125.
      - Kernel12 (edge a1 vs facet b2): x12 = (k02 over a1's 2 endpoints) minus
        (k11 over b2's 3 edges) - boolean3.cpp:236, 255.
    Thesis remark (p.66): X02 and X20 ARE the winding number of a vertex in a
    facet; X03/X30 the winding in the full 3D shape.  So winding is the same
    boundary-sum machinery at the top level.

(iii) BACKWARD-HALFEDGE ANTISYMMETRY (thesis p.66): S or X on a backward half-edge
    = MINUS the value on the edge.  Manifold: LoadFaceEdges canonicalizes each
    half-edge to (start<end) and flips a Pair with isForward=false
    (boolean3.cpp:44-49), and the isForward sign factors (boolean3.cpp:183, 255)
    apply the minus.  This is what makes shared mesh edges cancel.

(iv) THEOREM 1 - INTERPOLATION ALWAYS POSSIBLE (thesis p.76-77).  Whenever
    X_ij != 0, among its level-(k-1) sub-terms there is ALWAYS at least one pair
    where "A shadows B" and one where "B shadows A", so the intersection point can
    be interpolated.  Proof is purely combinatorial: if one type were missing, the
    boundary-sum formula would FORCE X_ij = 0 (contradiction), using only the sum
    structure + antisymmetry + "A and B adhere to the topological constraints"
    (each is a closed oriented manifold).  Manifold: the "either the left or right
    must shadow, but not both" invariant and the DEBUG_ASSERT(k == 2)
    (boolean3.cpp:98-100, 138, 194, 272).

(v) THEOREM 2 - THE CLOSURE ITSELF (thesis p.77): every composite edge at level 4
    has EXACTLY equal numbers of start- and end-vertices (E_ij = 0).  E_ij is a
    sum of net end-vertex counts that telescopes to zero by the boundary structure.
    This is the guarantee that the intersection polylines CLOSE: indeg == outdeg
    everywhere, so the seam breaks into well-formed edges and the result is a valid
    closed 2-manifold.  Thesis summary p.1: "topologically robust ... guaranteed
    to generate a topologically valid result from topologically valid input,
    IRRESPECTIVE of numerical errors."

WHAT MAKES IT ROBUST.  Theorems 1 and 2 are proved from (a) the boundary-sum
DEFINITION of X, (b) antisymmetry, and (c) the input being closed oriented
manifolds.  They use ZERO facts about the numerical CORRECTNESS of any shadow
value.  So ANY assignment of the shadow signs that is (1) CONSISTENT (the same
S_ij(oA,oB) evaluates identically wherever that pair appears) and (2) ANTISYMMETRIC
under half-edge reversal produces closed output.  Property (2) is structural.
Property (1) is FREE because every shadow is an exact 1D compare (iv above) - the
same (p,q,dir) always yields the same bit.  Wrong signs just realize the EXACT
answer for a slightly PERTURBED pair (P shifted +eps*n, Q shifted -eps*n), which is
a genuine realizable configuration, so its arrangement closes.

WHAT BIPARTITENESS EXACTLY BUYS (separating it from 1D-exactness):

  - 1D-EXACTNESS buys CONSISTENCY and the ABSENCE of an uncertain regime.  Every
    decision is a monotone compare of two INPUT coordinates; doubles compare
    exactly; the only degeneracy is exact equality.

  - BIPARTITENESS buys (A) a GLOBAL 2-element perturbation order: "B shifts +axis"
    is assignable because every predicate has a canonical A-side and B-side, and
    the perturbation direction is a global function of the FEATURE (its operand
    label), not of the pair.  This is the expandP convention (boolean3.cpp:475-481:
    Union expands both, Difference/Intersection contracts P expands Q).  And (B) a
    BIGRADED (double-complex) boundary structure: partial_A and partial_B are
    independent operators, which is what lets the telescoping in Theorem 2 cancel.

  - GEOMETRICALLY, the payoff of both together: the seam of two SIMPLE closed
    manifolds P and Q is generically a 1-MANIFOLD (disjoint closed loops, every
    vertex degree 2).  A branch point would need >=3 sheets at one point; with only
    two simple surfaces that requires P tangent to Q (or a coincidence), which is
    CODIMENSION 1 = non-generic, and the expandP perturbation REMOVES it.  So under
    SoS the boolean3 seam is ALWAYS degree-2, and Theorem 2's balanced 1-cycle is a
    union of simple loops, each of which bounds automatically.  Closure is free.

2. THE CONSTRUCTION ATTEMPT FOR SELF-OVERLAP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Setup.  One oriented soup S.  The seam graph is S crossing ITSELF: RecordSeams
(overlap3.cpp:1383) enumerates non-adjacent face-pair crossings (skipping
shared-vertex self-adjacent pairs), each seam carrying its PARTNER plane
(BuildSeam.other, overlap3.cpp:1304-1311).  Two seams on face f with distinct
partner planes crossing in f's interior name a 3-face TRIPLE POINT {f,g,h}
(overlap3.cpp:1307-1310) - the junction registry's branch vertices.

The shadow relation now has to compare feature f of S against feature g of S.  The
"expand P, contract Q" convention has no referent: f and g are the same surface.
Two formulations to recover it:

FORMULATION (a): SPLIT EACH SELF-CROSSING BY GLOBAL INDEX (simulate bipartiteness).
  Give the pair (f,g) an A-role/B-role by min/max global vertex index; perturb by
  index (Edelsbrunner-Mucke).  This IS a globally consistent, realizable
  perturbation: a total order on features by index is a genuine infinitesimal point
  displacement.  It closes the EXACT-TIE part of the problem.
  --> This is ALREADY BUILT: docs/Regularize3D.md:233-249 (stage-6 single global
      SoS, Orient3DExactSign keyed by global vertex index, TOTAL by construction).
  WHERE IT DOES NOT REACH: index-splitting fixes tie-breaking (orient == 0), not
  the UNCERTAIN-NONZERO regime.  boolean3 has no uncertain regime because its
  shadows are exact 1D compares.  The self-resolver's branch-point decisions are
  orient2d on CONSTRUCTED near-tangent points (seam-seam crossings), which in
  double have THREE regimes: +, -, and filter-uncertain-but-genuinely-nonzero.  SoS
  only decides the exact zero.  The uncertain-nonzero case is FORM-DEPENDENT: the
  same fact ("triple point T is left of seam s") computed from face f's projection
  vs face g's projection ROUNDS to different signs.  Measured directly:
  f4-junction-1784124820.md:99 - a per-face 2D-frame on-segment test gives
  t=0.9999 in one incident face's frame and t=1.0001 in the other's, MANUFACTURING
  a spurious T-junction.  f4-design-a-1784117012.md section 8 - three independent
  per-face RemoveOverlaps2D arrangements at each triple point DISAGREE on which
  sub-faces exist (open fans 121 -> 126 under a retention change, still open); the
  dominant residue (~116/121) is "arrangement-level radial inconsistency," not a
  tie and not a retention rule.
  So (a) reproduces bipartiteness's COMBINATORIAL orientation and closes ties, but
  the seam-seam crossing has no exact 1D-shadow form, so form-dependence survives.
  RESULT: closure NOT achieved for any consistent-but-approximate sign assignment
  once branch points are present.

FORMULATION (b): DECIDE-BY-CONVENTION + COCYCLE-REPAIR ON OPEN LOOPS.
  Make arbitrary consistent radial choices, emit, detect the loops that fail to
  close (the unbalanced fans), and repair by synthesizing closing faces.
  --> This was RUN as a measurement: attack22-c3-1784146879.md.  Findings:
      - The emitted open boundary is a BALANCED signed 1-cycle: indeg == outdeg at
        EVERY vertex (43 dangling half-edges, 20 welded verts, degree hist
        {2:7,4:7,6:4,8:1,12:1}).  So the self-ANALOG of Theorem 2 (boundary of a
        boundary is zero, the 1-cycle balances) DOES hold for ANY consistent
        assignment.  Good news, and the deep reason the trade LOOKS available.
      - But the balanced 1-cycle carries 13 BRANCH NODES (degree up to 12).  A
        balanced 1-cycle with degree->2 nodes is NOT a disjoint union of simple
        loops; it bounds a 2-manifold only if the radial pairing at each branch is
        consistent AND the bounded sub-faces are representable.
      - The repair FAILS.  Every winding-validated candidate closing face is either
        (comp0, 9 edges) OUTSIDE every input plane = distortion, banned by the
        witness theorem (output = boundary of {w_S>=1}, no invented geometry), or
        (comp1-4, 13 edges) over-fills a NON-UNIFORM winding region the production
        winding REJECTS.  Trajectory measured 22 open -> 22 open.  Nothing ships.
  RESULT: (b) is obstructed by representability + the witness theorem.  The
  1-cycle balances (cocycle holds) but does NOT bound a representable surface.

THE OBSTRUCTION, STATED PRECISELY (the odd-cycle analog).  The concrete
counterexample is a GENERIC TRIPLE POINT: three sheets A,B,C of the SAME surface S
meeting at p.  Three surfaces in 3D meet at isolated triple points generically
(3 equations, 3 unknowns) and the point PERSISTS under any perturbation of S,
because A,B,C are three regions of ONE perturbed surface, not three independent
objects to pull apart.  Contrast the bipartite case, where a branch point requires
P tangent to Q (codim 1) and SoS removes it.  So:

    Theorem-2's "balanced 1-cycle" HOLDS in the self case (attack22 measured it).
    Theorem-2's bipartite COROLLARY "balanced 1-cycle => bounds a closed
    2-manifold" FAILS, because self-incidence produces IRREMOVABLE degree->2 branch
    nodes, and closing a fan at a branch node needs a radial/sub-face decision that
    is (i) an orient2d on constructed points with a form-dependent uncertain regime,
    and (ii) required to AGREE across all incident faces.  Per-face double
    arrangements disagree there (measured), so the fan opens = non-manifold.

This is the "harder for the self-intersect case" the owner suspected, made exact.
The corpus confirms the localization: docs/Regularize3D.md:143-146,184-185 - every
resolving carrier forces ZERO triple points (each arrangement edge is exactly two
distinct planes, "the PairUp shape," radial sort a trivial 2-element sort from
input normals at level 0); openscad's large dirty component is the only carrier
that is triple-point dense (f4-design-a:16-19, ~151 distinct triple points).  The
wall lands exactly where branch points appear, nowhere else.

REFINEMENT (honest, from attack22-c3): the branch-node radial ORDER is NOT itself
the wall.  attack22-c3 proved the cyclic order around each branch node is decidable
at LEVEL 0 from input normals + the emitted fwd bit (0 genuine ties on 10/10 fans,
13/13 nodes orderable), extending the 2-sheet input-normal sort to the through-
plane double-sheet case.  The surviving wall is the missing sub-face EXTENT: the
near-tangent seam crossings bound sub-faces whose extent runs from ~1e-4 down to
~2eps, which the per-face eps-merge collapses into one cell spanning two winding
regions.  So the obstruction is TWO nested walls, both branch-point-induced:
  W1 CONSISTENCY: per-face double arrangements disagree at the branch point ->
     open fans.  Closable by ONE exact arrangement shared across incident faces
     (orient2d on constructed points = the kernel tripwire).
  W2 REPRESENTABILITY: even exactly arranged, sub-eps sub-faces collapse at
     double rounding (the thin-cell wall, docs stage 7).

3. THE FEEDBACK PROBLEM (does a wrong sign corrupt the winding cocycle?)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Question: in boolean3, winding/retention comes from FIXED operand manifolds +
crossing parities.  In ours, w_S is computed from the SAME decided crossings.  Can
a wrong-but-locally-consistent sign assignment change an integer winding value and
violate path-independence?

ANSWER: NO.  The winding is DECOUPLED from the arrangement decisions, so the
retention side is not where the trade breaks.  Two facts:

(i) boolean3's winding IS coupled to the crossings (Winding03_, boolean3.cpp:387-
    459): it unites vertices across UNBROKEN edges via DisjointSets (an edge is
    "broken" iff it is in the crossing set p1q2, boolean3.cpp:407-412), seeds one
    ray per component, counts s02 against the FIXED opposite manifold
    (boolean3.cpp:444), and floods.  But it is STILL cocycle-safe, because the
    crossing set is the Theorem-2-closed intersection: the winding delta across any
    crossing equals the recorded s02, and the closed seam makes the field
    single-valued.  Coupled, but safe by the closure theorem.

(ii) OUR winding is NOT coupled to the arrangement at all.  WindingAt
    (overlap3.cpp:1226-1238) loops over ALL input triangles and ray-casts p->seed
    through WindCrossTri (overlap3.cpp:1096-1119, filter-then-exact); it reads
    in.vertPos_ / in.halfedge_ ONLY (overlap3.cpp:1231-1233) and NEVER touches a
    seam, a decided crossing, or the arrangement.  RobustWinding
    (overlap3.cpp:1245-1252) just retries seeds.  So the integer winding VALUE of a
    probe point is a pure function of the FIXED input soup - a valid oriented
    2-cycle - and by the SAME Theorem-2 closure the ray crossing count is a
    cocycle: docs/ExactArrangement3D.md:490-493 (path-independence is a cocycle
    invariant for a closed oriented 2-cycle; deltas sum to zero around any loop)
    and 612-628 (measured: cocycle-unanimous across independent exterior seed rays,
    path-independence violations zero).  The deltas are +-1 integers, never
    near-zero, so the winding half is FP-safe by construction
    (docs/Regularize3D.md:130-134).

CONSEQUENCE.  A wrong-but-consistent arrangement sign CANNOT change an integer
winding value or violate the cocycle - retention is NOT ill-defined.  What a wrong
sign CAN do is (1) place a sub-cell centroid probe on the wrong side of a seam,
misclassifying WHICH sub-region to emit (a boundary-representation error, not a
winding error), and (2) fail to close the emission fan (section 2, W1).  So the
obstruction is entirely on the ARRANGEMENT side (the cell/seam graph whose boundary
we emit), NOT on the winding side.

This SHARPENS the verdict: the trade is not blocked by "winding becomes ambiguous"
(the failure mode one might fear).  It is blocked by "the boundary 1-cycle, though
balanced, does not bound a representable 2-manifold at branch points."  The winding
oracle is exactly why zero-oracle-wrong holds even at the fail: every candidate
completion face in attack22-c3 was winding-VALIDATED and rejected, proving the
residue is a representability wall, not a mislabeling.

4. LITERATURE ANCHOR (from knowledge; flag uncertainty)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Confident (recalled, not fetched):

  - Smith-Dodgson (this thesis; also Smith & Dodgson, CAD 2007, "A topologically
    robust algorithm for Boolean operations ... using approximate arithmetic") is
    topologically-robust-APPROXIMATE but for the BIPARTITE 3D Boolean (two valid
    shapes) and for 2D SIMPLIFICATION of a single self-overlapping polygon.  It
    does NOT give a 3D SELF-intersection resolver.  The gap is telling: the robust-
    approximate result exists where the structure is bipartite (3D) or where the
    branch structure is 1-dimensional and orderable (2D), i.e. exactly where my
    obstruction does not bite.

  - The mesh-arrangements line went EXACT precisely at self/multi-way arrangements:
    Zhou, Grinspun, Zorin, Panozzo, "Mesh Arrangements for Solid Geometry"
    (SIGGRAPH 2016) - exact predicates (rational / interval + exact fallback,
    libigl/CGAL), arbitrary self-intersection + non-manifold input, then
    winding-number cell classification.  Cherchi, Pellacini, Attene, Livesu, "Fast
    and Robust Mesh Arrangements using Floating-point Arithmetic" (SIGGRAPH Asia
    2020) - uses Attene's INDIRECT PREDICATES ("Indirect Predicates for Geometric
    Constructions," 2020): exact orient predicates on IMPLICIT (constructed)
    intersection points WITHOUT materializing them.  That is precisely the
    campaign's priced tripwire (docs/Regularize3D.md E1 SPEC, lines 581-590; the
    {f,g,h} plane-triple symbolic route) and precisely my W1.  These are the
    field-standard self-overlap resolvers, and they are EXACT-at-the-branch-point.

  - Generalized winding number line: Jacobson, Kavan, Sorkine, "Robust Inside-
    Outside Segmentation using Generalized Winding Numbers" (SIGGRAPH 2013).
    Robust-APPROXIMATE and self-overlap-tolerant, but it REMESHES (tetrahedralize +
    graph-cut on a soft winding field) rather than emitting the input faces - it
    gives up input-face fidelity, and near thin/branch features the extracted
    boundary can be topologically wrong.  That is exactly the "distortion, banned
    by the witness theorem" this campaign rejects (attack22-c3, comp0).

Honest synthesis / what I know: I am not aware of any published method that is BOTH
input-face-faithful AND topologically-robust-APPROXIMATE for general 3D self-
overlap.  The faithful ones (Zhou, Cherchi) went exact; the robust-approximate ones
(GWN + extraction) went re-meshed.  My section-2 obstruction explains the dichotomy:
the branch-point radial/sub-face decision has no consistent float form ON THE INPUT
FACES, so faithfulness forces exactness there.

SPECULATION (flag): whether a bounded-perturbation "snap the branch neighborhood to
a shared exact micro-arrangement, accept O(eps) geometry" could give faithful-and-
approximate at branch points is, to my knowledge, open; it is essentially the
indirect-predicates route with the exact evaluation replaced by a consistency-only
snap, and the attack22 evidence (position-only once-only measured insufficient,
f4-design-a section 8) is against it, but I have not seen it ruled out in general.

5. VERDICT FOR THE OWNER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

WHAT WE ALREADY HOLD (so the verdict is about the UNCERTAIN-sign regime only):
  - SoS = the trade for EXACT ties: one global Edelsbrunner-Mucke convention keyed
    by vertex index, realizable by infinitesimality, TOTAL by construction
    (docs/Regularize3D.md:233-249).  This is formulation (a)'s realizable part, and
    it is DONE.
  - The winding half: FP-safe integer deltas, decoupled ray-cast, cocycle-unanimous
    (section 3).  Retention is never ill-defined.
  - Sub-eps output: fails closed (thin-cell, docs stage 7), never a sliver.
  - CONSEQUENCE: on the 2-sheet class (no triple points) the trade IS available and
    REALIZED - eps-noisy geometry, exact topology, closure free - which is boolean3's
    trade.  The entire corpus except openscad lives here.

VERDICT: (b) OBSTRUCTED for the >=3-sheet branch-point regime, exact-or-refuse is
theorem-backed THERE.  The obstruction is concrete (a generic triple point / the
openscad balanced-1-cycle), and it is NOT a winding failure and NOT a tie-breaking
failure - it is that boolean3's closure guarantee is a BIPARTITE property
(non-generic branch points, perturbed away) that self-incidence structurally
violates (generic branch points, un-perturbable).  The balanced-1-cycle analog of
Theorem 2 holds; the "therefore it bounds" corollary does not.

What the trade WOULD buy if the owner crosses the tripwire (the W1 cost): a
refuse-NEVER mode on branch-point carriers, at the price of ONE exact predicate
FORM - orient2d on the constructed near-tangent crossing, evaluated symbolically on
the {f,g,h} plane triple over the existing Orient3DExactSign accumulator, ~150-250
LOC (attack22-c3 "PRICE"; f4-design-a Part 3), or vendored Shewchuk predicates.c as
the fallback.  Even then W2 (sub-eps sub-face representability) remains, so
"refuse-never" is not fully reachable without also defining representability-aware
rounding in exact space (stage 7).  So: the exact kernel at the branch point is not
an implementation shortcut the owner is avoiding - it is forced by the geometry.

Both outcomes are the win the task wanted: the exact-or-refuse posture at branch
points is now THEOREM-BACKED (bipartite closure has no self-analog there), and the
approximate-topology trade is characterized as AVAILABLE-AND-HELD on the entire
2-sheet class.
