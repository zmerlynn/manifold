## 0. WHAT F4 IS (the measured ground, not re-derived)

F4 = EmitSeamedFace branch B3 (overlap3.cpp L1848): RemoveOverlaps2D produces a
2D arrangement vertex with NO input preimage = a constructed seam-seam crossing =
a genuine 3-face TRIPLE POINT (the point lies on planes f, g, h). openscad's large
dirty component is triple-point dense (131 faces, ~151 distinct triple points,
453 incidences). The pos2in bit-exact input-preimage lookup refuses ANY seamed
face whose 2D arrangement has a constructed crossing, so openscad fails closed.

The bare completion prototype (getOrAdd2D: accept the crossing, canon3 =
per-face plane-frame back-projection) dissolves F4 and reveals the DEEPER wall:
NonManifoldEmission "unresolvable sheet contact" = an UNBALANCED FAN at
SplitTouchingSheets (overlap3.cpp L84, `fwd.size() != bwd.size()`). Measured:
121 open-boundary edges, 8 radial ties, 1 overlap; only 5 weld twins in
(eps,16eps]; w_above spans [-2,+3] with 482 negative cells. The residual has
THREE candidate mechanisms; this lane MEASURED their split (section 8):
  R1  per-face triple point reconstructed 3x (3 double-rounded images) -> the
      5 near-parallel weld twins.  A MINORITY (~5 of 121).
  R2  the mult-1 emission rule (retain iff w_above==0) wrong where the local
      winding jump != 1.  The report GUESSED this dominant; this lane REFUTES it
      (winding-jump emission moves 121 -> 126, section 8).  ~0 of 121.
  R3  ARRANGEMENT-LEVEL radial inconsistency: the three incident faces build
      independent double 2D arrangements, so the sub-face structure around each
      triple point disagrees and the seam-segment edges have no partner.  The
      DOMINANT mechanism (~116 of 121) - and the one that needs the exact
      substrate (Part 3), NOT a retention rule.  = the report's RESIDUAL-1 /
      plane-based representation.

## 1. THE GRAFT (three parts, smallest sound completion)

### Part 1 - ONCE-ONLY triple point keyed by the plane triple (kills R1 by
construction)

MECHANISM. A triple point T = plane(f) INT plane(g) INT plane(h) must have ONE
3D image shared by all three incident seamed faces, so the emitted sub-faces weld
bit-identically. Today the three faces each let RemoveOverlaps2D construct the
crossing in their own plane and back-project it (three rounded doubles). The graft
constructs T ONCE, memoized by the sorted canonical plane triple, and every face
uses that one double.

DATA STRUCTURES (overlap3.cpp, resolver scope).
  - BuildSeam gains provenance: the OTHER face id whose plane cut this seam.
      struct BuildSeam { vec3 p0, p1; int src; };   // +1 field
    RecordSeams already knows `src` at the point it pushes each seam (the pierced
    or piercing partner face of the pair loop), so this is a field write, not new
    computation.
  - A canonical PLANE id per face: coincident faces share a plane, so the triple
    point of three PLANES is keyed once even when reached through different face
    representatives.  planeId[f] = face2cluster[f] >= 0 ? clusterPlaneBase +
    face2cluster[f] : f.  (Coplanar members already share face2cluster; the base
    offset keeps cluster ids disjoint from face ids.)  A small std::vector<int>.
  - The cross-face cache (EmitComponentBoundary scope, built once, passed const&
    into EmitSeamedFace):
      std::map<std::array<int,3>, vec3> triplePts;   // sorted planeId triple -> T
    Populated by a PRE-PASS (below); read-only during per-face emission so the
    per-face parallelism the operator promises is preserved.

PRE-PASS (once, before the per-face EmitSeamedFace loop).  For each seamed face f,
for each unordered pair of its seams (s_g with src g, s_h with src h, g!=h), test
whether the two seam segments cross in the INTERIOR of f (a level-0 orient2d
segment-segment straddle on the seam endpoints already projected in f's frame -
input-derived, filter-decidable).  On a crossing, key = sort{planeId[f],
planeId[g], planeId[h]}; if absent, compute T once as the 3-plane intersection
(one canonical derivation - Cramer on the three unnormalized face normals and
offsets, in double) and insert.  ~80-120 LOC.

INJECTION (in EmitSeamedFace, replacing the getOrAdd2D back-projection).  Before
calling RemoveOverlaps2D, split each seam of f at the projections of its triple
points (proj_f(T) for every {f,g,h} in the cache incident to that seam), and
add proj_f(T) as an explicit arrangement vertex whose canon3 IS the memoized T.
Then every arrangement vertex has an input preimage (an original endpoint OR an
injected proj_f(T)), the EXISTING pos2in loop succeeds unchanged (no getOrAdd2D,
no B3 refusal), and canon3[that vertex] = the once-only T - bit-identical across
the three faces.  This is STRICTLY BETTER than the completion prototype: the
prototype back-projects RemoveOverlaps2D's own rounded per-face crossing (R1); the
graft feeds the shared T in, so all three faces weld on the same double.

WHY NO EXACT PREDICATE HERE.  Identifying which two planes concur at a crossing is
COMBINATORIAL (seam provenance), not geometric.  Computing T is a once-only
construction (output-side; the coordinate is eps-noisy by contract, only its
SHARING must be exact, which memoization gives for free).  The only geometric
decision is "do these two seams cross inside f" - a level-0 orient2d on
input-derived seam endpoints, filter-first.  On openscad the crossings are
macro-separated (measured nearMiss=0, 114/131 > 1e4*eps), so the filter certifies
every one; the near-degenerate tail routes to Part 3's gate.

### Part 2 - GENERAL WINDING-JUMP emission (NECESSARY once the arrangement is
exact; INERT before it - measured, section 8)

NOTE UP FRONT: this lane MEASURED that Part 2 alone does NOT close openscad (121
-> 126 open fans on the double, per-face, radially-inconsistent arrangement).  It
is a strict, corpus-safe generalization that becomes LOAD-BEARING only after
Parts 1+3 make the arrangement consistent; on an inconsistent arrangement it is
inert-to-slightly-harmful.  It is retained in the design because the final
consistent arrangement genuinely has jump!=1 cells (the everted/coincident
strata, w_above in [-2,+3]); it is sequenced LAST.

MECHANISM. Replace EmitSeamedFace's mult-1 test (measure w_above once, retain iff
== 0) with the SAME measure-both-sides witness rule FoldCoplanarClusters already
runs and pins (overlap3.cpp L2103-2124): measure the coupled winding on BOTH
sides of the sub-cell centroid, retain iff EXACTLY one side is inside {w_S>=1},
and orient by the solid side.

      const optional<int> wa = RobustWinding(in, cen3 + eps*nHat, seeds);
      const optional<int> wb = RobustWinding(in, cen3 - eps*nHat, seeds);
      if (!wa || !wb) { ok = false; return; }              // SoS axis, fail closed
      const bool aboveIn = *wa >= 1, belowIn = *wb >= 1;
      if (aboveIn == belowIn) continue;                    // not a boundary
      for (t : tris) push(belowIn ? {x,y,z} : {x,z,y});    // solid side -> orient

STRICT GENERALIZATION, not a flip.  For a genuinely mult-1 seamed sub-cell the
jump is +1 (wb = wa+1): aboveIn!=belowIn iff wa<=0 && wa+1>=1 iff wa==0, and
belowIn is true so the orientation is original - BITWISE-IDENTICAL to today's
rule.  The two rules differ ONLY where the measured jump != 1 (the everted and
coincident-sheet strata openscad forces).  So this is a no-op on every mult-1
carrier (siA/siB/... keep their bitwise resolves) and captures exactly the
dropped-boundary cells.  It is NOT the "simple orientation flip" s7b refuted (that
kept wa==0 and flipped); it is the landed, self-checked coplanar rule ported to
the transversal path.

~15-25 LOC (a straight port of the fold's block).

### Part 3 - THE PREDICATE SUBSTRATE (chosen: A, vendored Shewchuk on input)

DECISION: OPTION A - vendored Shewchuk expansions computing the degree-N
predicate directly on INPUT doubles; the constructed triple point NEVER
materializes in a decision.  Its POSITION is memoized once (Part 1) for OUTPUT and
welding only.  REJECT option B (exact rational T memoized and read by predicates).

Justification, against BOTH stated criteria:
  - TRIPWIRE TEXT ("vendor Shewchuk, never rebuild expansion arithmetic
    piecemeal", overlap3.cpp L703, docs open list).  A is the literally-named
    blessed route.  B needs either a hand-rolled bignum/rational (the piecemeal
    the tripwire bans) or a growth of the existing adaptive-integer accumulator to
    degree-N Cramer-ratio comparisons (NEW arithmetic, not a new caller - the
    reg3d-c2bx relaxation blesses new CALLERS with ZERO new arithmetic, which B
    violates).  B would also vendor the WRONG library (a bignum, not Shewchuk).
  - memoize-values / decide-predicates-from-realizable-geometry.  The campaign's
    OWN practice is "every combinatorial decision restructures to a level-0 INPUT
    predicate; constructed-point precision dissolved FOR DECISIONS"
    (docs "What is proven", S2) and "each triple point constructed ONCE via a
    single canonical derivation and referenced everywhere" (resolver mechanism).
    That IS option A: memoize the VALUE (the double position, for output/weld),
    decide predicates on the realizable INPUT geometry (the planes that realize
    T).  B does the opposite - it makes the constructed VALUE load-bearing for
    decisions, the exact thing "precision dissolved for decisions" rejects, and
    its rational coordinates CHAIN-blow-up (a triple point built from a triple
    point) whereas A stays degree-N in the original input forever.  A is CGAL's
    EPIC discipline (exact predicates, inexact constructions), the field standard.

WHAT A COSTS, and why openscad DOES force it (measured, section 8).  I initially
scoped the substrate as a backstop for a rare near-degenerate tail, on the theory
that openscad's macro-separated triple points (nearMiss=0) would let the double
arrangement carry it.  THE PROTOTYPE REFUTED THAT: even macro-separated triple
points are arranged INDEPENDENTLY by three per-face RemoveOverlaps2D calls, so the
radial structure around T disagrees across the three faces and the fans stay open
(section 8).  The exact substrate is therefore LOAD-BEARING, not a backstop: it is
what makes the three faces agree on ONE arrangement around each T.  It has two
concrete jobs:
  1. RADIAL CONSISTENCY (the load-bearing job).  Instead of three independent
     per-face RemoveOverlaps2D calls each rounding the crossing, decide the
     cyclic order of the seams around T, and the sub-face incidences, from the
     EXACT input-symbolic geometry (orient2d of the seam DIRECTION vectors and
     of T vs each other seam, all polynomials in the input plane coefficients,
     evaluated exactly by vendored Shewchuk expansions - T never materialized).
     Because all three faces evaluate the SAME input-symbolic predicates, they
     agree on the radial structure and the seam-segment edges pair.  This is what
     the double per-face arrangement cannot supply (section 8).
  2. NEAR-DEGENERATE SEPARATION (the tail guard).  A T within eps of a third
     seam / a triangle edge / another T: the exact predicates decide its side;
     absent the vendored kernel, a level-0 filter-first GATE certifies the
     well-separated case and FAILS CLOSED (new named reason) on the uncertain
     tail - the same never-guess posture as the EG-guard / phantom-seam guard
     (orient-attack F2), never a silent wrong resolve.

COST: ~200-400 LOC = vendored predicates.c (public-domain, zero hand-rebuilt
arithmetic) + the specific input-symbolic degree-N determinants for the radial
predicates + a propagated Edelsbrunner-Mucke perturbation on the input vertices
for the measure-zero 4-plane concurrency (the constructed-point SoS).  This is the
ONE tripwire decision the report priced.  Parts 1+2 landed WITHOUT it are a
corpus-safe NARROWING (F4 dissolved, new near-degenerate fail-closed), not
closure - measured (section 8): they leave ~116 open fans.

## 6. SOUNDNESS - w in {negative} cells under {w_S>=1} retention (everted sheets)

The retention criterion is d{w_S>=1} = the locus where one side of the sheet has
w>=1 and the other has w<1.  The measure-both-sides rule reads this DIRECTLY:
retain iff (wa>=1) != (wb>=1).  It makes NO assumption about the sign or magnitude
of the jump, so it is correct for any integer winding field, negative included:
  - jump +1, wa=0,wb=1: keep (the ordinary boundary).  Bitwise = old rule.
  - jump +1, wa=-1,wb=0 OR wa=-2,wb=-1: BOTH sides exterior (w<1), aboveIn ==
    belowIn == false, DROP.  Correct - an everted single sheet buried in the
    exterior is not on the solid boundary.  (This is the "negative w_above drops,
    not fails closed" the current comment already asserts for mult-1; the general
    rule preserves it.)
  - jump +2, wa=-1,wb=1: aboveIn=false, belowIn=true, KEEP.  Correct - a
    double-covered sheet whose two crossings straddle 1 (the everted-plus-buried
    corner the mult-1 rule WRONGLY dropped, the dominant R2 hole).
  - jump 0 or +-2 with both sides same class (wa=1,wb=1 or wa=0,wb=0): aboveIn ==
    belowIn, DROP.  Correct - no boundary crosses.
ORIENTATION under evertion: the solid side fixes the outward normal.  belowIn
(solid on the -nHat side) keeps the CCW-2D -> +nHat mapping (normal points to the
exterior +nHat side); aboveIn reverses.  This is the fold's pinned convention,
sound regardless of whether the face's own normal agrees with the winding gradient
(an everted face has its normal opposed to the gradient; the rule reads the gradient
from the two measured windings, not from the face normal, so evertion cannot flip
the answer).

The one safety property LOST versus the fold: FoldCoplanarClusters cross-checks
`wb - wa == m` against an INDEPENDENT in-plane cover m; the seamed path has no
independent m (the jump IS wb-wa), so a winding MISREAD is not caught by a
self-check - only by the re-gate.  This is acceptable (the re-gate is the standing
backstop) but it is a genuine reduction in defense-in-depth, recorded as Risk 2.

## 7. TOP 3 WAYS THIS DESIGN FAILS (adversarial, self-attack)

RISK 1 - CONFIRMED-FIRED (the falsifier).  The wall is arrangement-level radial
inconsistency (R3), which needs the exact substrate, NOT a retention rule.
Measured (section 8): winding-jump emission moves 121 -> 126 open fans, and the
report's own data says only 5/121 are position twins.  So the minimality thesis
("close openscad with Parts 1+2, no exact substrate") is REFUTED.  Part 1's
positional once-only is necessary but not sufficient (C-5: the reentrant corner
has three cross-phase constructions; positional dedup does not make the radial
structure agree - only ONE canonical exact construction shared across phases
does).  The honest terminal is "vendor Shewchuk" - the substrate is load-bearing,
not a backstop.  This risk did not just threaten the design; it MATERIALIZED, and
section 9 records the revised (substrate-first) verdict.

RISK 2 - THE SEAMED WINDING JUMP IS NOT ACTUALLY +-SMALL / THE PROBE GRAZES.
Part 2 measures wb-wa at a sub-cell centroid near a triple point.  Close to T the
eps-band (cen3 +- eps*nHat) can straddle a THIRD sheet, so wb-wa is not the
face's own contribution and the "jump" is an artifact of probe placement, not
geometry - the general rule would then KEEP or DROP wrongly.  Worse, near-tangent
shallow-dihedral openscad faces are the GT7081 winding-probe class: RobustWinding
returns nullopt (filter-uncertain) and the face FAILS CLOSED (the reg3d-c2bx
escalation covers WindingAt's da terms but the report measured openscad's winding
probe fires ZERO times today, so this path is untested on it).  If many centroids
graze, Part 2 fails closed and openscad stays unresolved.  Mitigation (present in
the fold, portable): classify at the largest-sub-triangle INCENTER + a generic
jitter (overlap3.cpp L2084-2097), not the centroid, to dodge axis-aligned grazes -
Part 2 should adopt the fold's incenter+jitter, not the seamed path's plain
centroid.  This is a real gap in my Part-2 sketch and would add ~10 LOC.

RISK 3 - THE RE-GATE DOES NOT BACKSTOP A WELD-MERGE FOLD (R1/R2(i) blind spot).
Even with once-only T, the emission still runs through BuildImpl's uniform
eps-weld.  If two GENUINELY DISTINCT triple points (or a triple point and an
input vertex) round within eps, the weld merges them, manufacturing a self-fold
that IsSelfIntersecting's shares-vertex skip is BLIND to (docs R1/R2(i)).
openscad is coplanar-dominated shallow-dihedral - the worst tail for sub-eps
distinct features - and the completion prototype already leaves 5 weld twins in
(eps,16eps].  So a wrong merge could pass the re-gate silently (worse than
fail-closed).  The separation gate (Part 3) is the intended guard, but it
certifies crossing-vs-SEAM separation, not crossing-vs-crossing weld separation
under the OUTPUT eps grid; extending it to the output weld is the R1 residual the
whole campaign leaves open.  My graft does not close R1's silent-merge class - it
inherits it.

## 9. VERDICT (honest, minimality thesis REFUTED)

The MINIMAL GRAFT does NOT close openscad.  Parts 1+2 (once-only position +
winding-jump emission, ZERO new predicate form) NARROW F4 - they dissolve the
triple-point refusal and are corpus-safe - but they leave ~116 open boundaries
that are arrangement-level radial inconsistency at the triple points, which no
retention rule and no position-only weld can pair.  Closing them REQUIRES the
three incident faces to share ONE consistent arrangement around each triple point,
which needs the exact substrate (Part 3, option A) to be LOAD-BEARING, not a
banked backstop:
  - the triple point's radial order in each face must be decided from the exact
    3-plane geometry (input-symbolic orient2d via vendored Shewchuk), so the three
    faces agree on which seam segment bounds which sub-face;
  - the injected proj_f(T) and the segment splits must be exact-consistent across
    the three faces (once-only is necessary but the report's C-5 shows positional
    once-only is not sufficient - the cross-phase construction must be canonical).

So my assigned angle (MINIMAL GRAFT) yields: Parts 1+2 are a real, small,
corpus-safe DOWN PAYMENT (they remove the triple-point refusal and add no
predicate), but they do NOT reach closure.  The load-bearing cost is Part 3's
substrate - and the substrate CHOICE (A: vendored Shewchuk, input-symbolic
