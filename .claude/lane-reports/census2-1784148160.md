    (attack22-proj-1784145001.md, re-read, not re-run). PlaneFrame::proj is the SOLE 2D

    JunctionSplitsOnSegment foot) still routes through rounded pf.proj.
    WHAT PROTECTS CORRECTNESS TODAY: on the RESOLVING carriers the emitted vertices are

    move the retained {w>=1} boundary. Measured: an unconditional exact axis-drop swap is

    a SILENT WRONG RESOLVE. Not demonstrated on any corpus carrier (the byte-diffs stay
    oracle-equivalent), but the guard is "canon3 verts + exact 3D winding", NOT "the
    projection is right" - so the liability is real and un-gated. This is the sharpest
    latent liability: the only masking is oracle tolerance on the resolving carriers.
