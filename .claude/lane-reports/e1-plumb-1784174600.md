## THE WORK (as commissioned)
Carry each seam segment's extent SYMBOLICALLY so "does crossing X lie within
segment S's extent" is decided EXACTLY, then swap the enumeration to
straddle-with-exact-extents.  KEY simplification (tripwire-respecting): the seam
segment on face f with partner g is exactly tri(f) INT tri(g), so
  X within seam(f,g) extent  <=>  X in tri(f) AND tri(g)
and the two seam SEGMENTS on f (partners g,h) properly cross iff their line
crossing X={f,g,h} is STRICTLY INTERIOR TO ALL THREE triangles f, g, h.  Each
membership is 3 orient2d of the constructed X against the triangle edges = the
LANDED degree-9 instantiation (2) - the endpoints' symbolic {f,g,neighbor}
plane-triple generators ARE those triangle edges.  This uses NO new predicate
FORM (a 1D-order-along-seam form would be a THIRD degree-8 instantiation = STOP);
the triangle-membership realization stays entirely within instantiation (2).
