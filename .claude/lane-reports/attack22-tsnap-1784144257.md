## VERDICT: TANGENCY SNAP REFUTED BY MEASUREMENT (more decisively than Route B)

The mission's premise is geometrically mis-stated by the measurement: there is no
near-tangent FACE-wall graze to snap. The |sin| 8e-5..8e-2 near-tangency is
between the two near-PARALLEL WALLS (wallSin), not between the face and a wall -
the face crosses the walls transversally at a median ~45deg (fwSin ~ 0.71).
Consequently the two "exact contact" moves the mission proposes both rotate the
face through a MACROSCOPIC angle:
  - "exact coplanar contact" (flatten f onto a wall so the crossing becomes a
    coplanar fold): 1.6e-5 .. 3.8 model units = 4.4e4x .. 1.1e10x eps. Not one of
    the 239 pairs is within 1000x eps. Making a ~45deg transversal face coplanar
    with a wall is a half-face-extent rotation, not a sub-eps nudge.
  - "exact collinear contact" (f contains wa INT wb so the two seams coincide):
    7e-3 .. 10 units = 2e7x .. 2.9e10x eps, WORSE - because wa,wb are near-
    PARALLEL, their meet-line is far away / ill-conditioned, so tilting f to
    contain it is enormous.
Both blow the eps-valid contract by 4-10 orders of magnitude. The near-incidence
quantity (nearest single vertex) is either exactly 0 (a shared corner, irrelevant
to the cell) or ~1e-5 (1e5x eps), and a single-vertex move neither makes f
coplanar nor collapses the thin cell - it manufactures a new transversal crossing
(the orient/routeB "near-tangent gets worse under snapping" fact).

WHY it is worse than Route B (and closes the whole INPUT-SIDE snap family): the
thin cell's generating near-tangency is a property of the WALL PAIR (near-
parallel), and its width lives in the wall-wall COINCIDENCE gap, which Route B
measured macroscopic (1e-5..2e-2). Route B moved a wall onto that gap (1e-5..2e-2);
r6 moved seam-graph vertices across it (closes only at 128*eps ~ 1e-8, breaks the
guard); this lane moves the transversal FACE, which is even farther from the gap
(1.6e-5..10). No input-side vertex snap - of walls, of seam vertices, or of the
near-tangent face - can close a MACROSCOPIC coincidence gap within eps. The
sub-eps thin cell is a near-tangent 2D-PROJECTION collapse (an orthonormal
projection of a ~1e-8..1e-4 3D separation onto a face whose normal absorbs it),
NOT a sub-eps INPUT coincidence any snap can round away.
