### The measured mechanism of the RemoveOverlaps2D wall (boolean2.cpp read)
RemoveOverlaps2D(vertsIn, edges, eps, ..., &sub):

 - SweepWinding: Smith sweep over the incidence-split sub-edges. <-- re-crosses the
   rounded sub-segments -> a NEW crossing vertex with no verts2 preimage (face-467 b3).
 - sub = per-input-edge subdivision chains.  EmitSeamedFace maps each sub position back

### The exact overlay (scoped, Option B - complete pre-split -> PSLG -> ExtractCells)
After EnumerateTriplePoints pre-splits every seam at its exact interior crossing
(seamTriples, once-only keyed by the 3D triple point) and BuildJunctionRegistry
pre-splits every emitted edge at every registry vertex interior to it, the pre-split
segment set has NO remaining interior crossing (proven per face-plane: tri-edge x
tri-edge meet only at corners; tri-edge x seam meet at the on-boundary seam endpoint
registry split; seam x seam cross only at registered triple points).  So for a flagged
face the arrangement is the pre-split PSLG itself: SKIP RemoveOverlaps2D (no eps-merge,
no sweep), build uedges from the pre-split edges directly, ExtractCells, both-sides
retain, emit.  The gap-free wedge crossing that the DEFAULT rounded-straddle gate
declines is what the flag ADDS (E1_PURE exact straddle minus the default set, minus the
14-eps collapse envelope).  Sub-eps wedge TIP cells collapse to their split point (the
hybrid stage-2 rule; the exact overlay IS the downstream that holds it).

Byte-clean rail: on resolving carriers the flag is EMPTY (zero gap-free chains - GT7081's
crossings are all <14 eps -> collapsed; aliases <eps -> collapsed), so every face keeps
the RemoveOverlaps2D path byte-identically.  Kernel: the TWO landed instantiations only.
