KEY per-face simplification (reg3d-s2 step 3): within a face the seam chords do NOT
  cross each other (zero triple points); endpoints are on a triangle edge or interior
  (dangling / chain junctions). So per-face is a set of NON-CROSSING polylines in a
  triangle - the sweep handles it, and cells are well-defined.
KEY winding reduction: crossing a seam (f,j) within f flips whether you are inside j on
  BOTH sides of f simultaneously, so w+ - w- is CONSTANT across f (= f's own delta).
  Retention "d{w>=1} on exactly one side" therefore depends only on w-(q) (or w+(q)),
  a 2D winding function of the seams -> maps to boolean2's WindRule IF the mults are
  oriented right. This is the S-mult viability argument; but zero-oracle-wrong says
  classify emitted geometry by REAL 3D WindingAt + re-gate + volume band regardless.
