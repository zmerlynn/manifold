### Analytic derivation (pre-empirical, to know what to measure)

Let L be the shared line, direction w. For witnesses x_i on sheet i and x_j on
sheet j (both off L), the code's orient3d(a,b,c,d) = det[a-d; b-d; c-d] (signed
tet volume, antisymmetric). For p,q on L (any two distinct points):

  f(x_i,x_j) := orient3d(p,q,x_i,x_j) = ((p-x_j) x (q-x_j)) . (x_i - x_j)
              = N_j . (x_i - p),   N_j := (q-p) x (x_j - p)

N_j is a normal to sheet j's plane (it contains p,q on L and x_j). So f = signed
side of sheet-j's-plane that witness x_i lies on. Equivalently
f = -(q-p) . ((x_i-p) x (x_j-p)) = the scalar-triple-product angular comparator
of the two witnesses' perpendicular directions around L. Two consequences to
MEASURE, not just assert:
  (a) base-point invariance: shifting p along L by s*w leaves f unchanged (the
      repeated-w minors vanish) -> p,q need not be input points; ANY two points
      on L (same direction) give the same sign. So the reduction is legitimately
      "on input points" whenever two input verts lie on L, and equals the pure
      input predicate orient3d(face_j_verts, x_i) otherwise (up to the fixed sign
      relating L's direction to face j's orientation).
  (b) witness invariance: sign(f) depends on x_i only through which side of
      sheet-j's-plane it is on. Sheet i crosses plane j along L, so all x_i on
      the SAME side of L within sheet i share the side -> witness-invariant.
      Coplanar sheets (i,j same plane) give f==0 (the fold's tie, not radial).

A single orient3d is a HALF-TURN comparator (cross-product sign); a full cyclic
sort needs a half-plane reference split. That is a USE detail for step 2, not a
refutation - measure whether the pairwise signs are consistent with SOME cyclic
order and match the exact-normal order.

THE REDUCTION HOLDS - verified three ways, zero-oracle-wrong:
- T1 algebraic identity: orient3d(p,q,x_i,x_j) == -(dir_i x dir_j) for L=z-axis,
  19903/19903 configs, witnesses at ARBITRARY heights along L. So the reduction
  IS the exact 2D angular cross comparator and is invariant to the witness's
  along-L (height) component.
- T2 general-position PENCIL (book-of-pages, the radial rule's real target),
  N=6 transversal sheets on a random line, 300 trials: 9000/9000 sheet-pairs
  the reduction sign == the EXACT projected angular cross (one global
  orientation); 144000/144000 witness pairs invariant (same-side); 300/300 the
  full cyclic radial order reconstructed from the reduction (half-split +
  within-half sort) == the exact angular order. The reduction is a correct,
  witness-invariant radial comparator wherever a book-of-pages exists.
- T3 REAL EntangledBars junction L={y=2,z=2}: p,q ARE input verts ((0,2,2),
  (6,2,2)). Non-coplanar pair A-wall vs A-cap -> single definite sign; the two
  COPLANAR z=2 caps -> orient3d==0 (the fold's tie, correctly NOT a radial
  decision); A-wall vs B-cap gives BOTH signs only because B-cap CROSSES L (a
  full plane through L = two rays) - split by side of L each side is a single
  sign. So witness-invariance holds exactly per the premise's "same side of the
  sheet" qualifier, and that qualifier is load-bearing (a sheet crossing L
  contributes two oppositely-signed rays, correctly).
