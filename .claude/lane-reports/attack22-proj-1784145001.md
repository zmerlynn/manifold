## HYPOTHESIS

The 22's "2D-collapse at ~1e-8" is (partly/wholly) PROJECTION ROUNDING, not
geometry. RemoveOverlaps2D consumes PlaneFrame-projected coords: proj(P) =
(dot(P-origin, e1), dot(P-origin, e2)), where e1/e2 are inexact unit vectors
from the normalized normal, so each 2D coordinate carries dot-product rounding.
An EXACT axis-drop projection (select the two non-dominant absolute coordinates
- pure coordinate SELECTION, no arithmetic) feeds the sweep's exact orient2d
predicates exact input/construction doubles at level 0. Phantom-x already proved
the rounded projection manufactures 6-7 phantom crossings at the triple-
enumeration layer; the overlay layer is bigger.

## CODE MODEL (confirmed by reading)

- PlaneFrame::proj is the SOLE 2D projection primitive on the seamed thin-cell
  path. Every projection consumer routes through it: verts2 (pf.add), pos2in
  key (verts2 bits), pushChain param sort, JunctionSplitsOnSegment on-line foot,
  and EnumerateTriplePoints' on-seam split position `x` (SegLineIntersect2D on
  pf.proj seg[]). proj is AFFINE, so swapping it propagates consistently.
- RemoveOverlaps2D: MergeVerts (union-find, |proj(Pi)-proj(Pj)| <= eps),
  BuildIncidenceLists (eps vertex-on-edge), then SweepWinding whose crossing
  tests are EXACT on the doubles it is given. So the sweep is exact ON the
  ROUNDED projected coords - the projection rounding is baked in upstream.
- cen3 reconstruction (a + cen2.x*e1 + cen2.y*e2) is the ONLY consumer that
  inverts the PlaneFrame basis; under axis-drop it must be the direct 3D
  centroid of the sub-triangle's canon3 points (projection-independent).
- kBary / EmitCleanFaces / FoldCoplanarClusters are the EXACTLY-COPLANAR regime
  (clean/fold clusters), NOT the seamed 22; left on PlaneFrame deliberately.

## STEP 1 - MEASURE (F4P_PROJ, per-face, both projections on the SAME 3D points)

For every seamed face on openscad's large dirty comp, compare crossing and
eps-merge decisions under PlaneFrame (exact orient2d on the rounded pf.proj
doubles = what the sweep decides) vs exact axis-drop (ExactSegProperCross /
ExactOrient2DDrop on the 3D coords). Both use the ONE blessed exact predicate
FORM; any disagreement is PURE projection rounding.

    large dirty comp (574 seamed faces, openEdges=22):
      CROSSINGS: xTotal=33231 xAgree=32745
                 xPFonly=319  (PlaneFrame over-detects: phantom crossings)
                 xADonly=167  (PlaneFrame MISSES a crossing axis-drop finds)
                 -> 486 crossing decisions DIFFER, across 268/574 faces
      EPS-MERGE: mPFmerge=76  coincident=72 (correct)
                 projCulprit=4   (3D-distinct AND axis-drop keeps distinct:
                                  PlaneFrame rounding manufactured the merge)
                 normalDrop=0    (NONE are normal-direction collapses)
                 mADonly=0       (axis-drop never merges a PF-distinct pair)
    small comp: 0 disagreements everywhere.

FINDINGS:
- The projection is NOT innocent at the overlay layer: 486 crossing decisions +
  4 spurious merges differ, all on the dirty component. The overlay layer IS
  bigger than phantom-x's 6-7 (as predicted).
- normalDrop=0 is decisive: at the MergeVerts vertex level the 22 are NOT
  "near-tangent normal-direction collapses" (axis-drop is non-expanding vs 3D:
  |dAD| <= |D3D|, so it NEVER breaks a genuine <=eps merge, and it preserves
  in-plane distance exactly, so it refutes PlaneFrame's rounding-only merges).
- The 486 changes are SPREAD over 268 faces; the 22 open edges sit on ~13
  faces. The projection errors are NOT concentrated on the failing faces - most
  are harmless. That alone argues projection is not the specific culprit.

## STEP 2 - PROTOTYPE THE SWAP (F4P_SWAP, unconditional axis-drop overlay)

Added an orientation-preserving exact axis-drop to PlaneFrame::proj (select the
two non-dominant absolute coords, swap them when the dropped normal component is
negative so 2D-CCW still maps to +nHat), enabled for EnumerateTriplePoints +
EmitSeamedFace only, with cen3 = direct 3D sub-tri centroid. Validation: the CCW
guard (0.5*cross(pb-pa,pc-pa)>0) passed on every seamed face (okfaces=574
unchanged, b3/b4/b5=0), so the parity fix is correct and no consumer broke.

OPEN-EDGE TRAJECTORY (openscad large dirty comp):
    default: openEdges=22  fan1=12 fan3=9 fan4=1 | atTriple=1 oneTriple=14 noTriple=7
    swap:    openEdges=21  fan1=14 fan3=6 fan4=1 | atTriple=4 oneTriple=10 noTriple=7
  Net -1. fan3 -3 / fan1 +2 / atTriple +3: the swap RELOCATES the fan imbalance
  (closes ~3 three-fans, opens ~2 one-fans elsewhere), it does not RESOLVE the
  component. Still fail-closed (NonManifoldEmission), openscad test PASSES.
  The 167 crossings PlaneFrame MISSED are now found under axis-drop, yet only 1
  net edge closes -> the fans are a winding/emission-balance problem at genuine
  near-tangent junctions, not a missed-crossing problem.

FULL SUITE with F4P_SWAP=1 (PAR=OFF, incl GT7081 + openscad): 32/32 PASSED

## STEP 3 - OTHER ROUNDED-FRAME CONSUMERS ON THE THIN-CELL PATH

- pos2in keying: verts2 bits. Under swap = axis-drop bits, keyed exactly;
  b3faces=0 under swap confirms every arrangement position still maps back.
- pushChain param sort + JunctionSplitsOnSegment foot: both via pf.proj -
  covered by the single proj swap (affine, so on-line feet stay on-line).
- seam parameterization (EnumerateTriplePoints split x): pf.proj - covered.
- interior-witness / RobustWinding: probes cen3 +/- eps*nHat; RobustWinding is
  exact on 3D input (projection-free) apart from cen3, which the swap fixes.
- kBary (EmitCleanFaces) / FoldCoplanarClusters cen3: the exactly-coplanar
  regime, not the 22; unaffected (their carriers stay byte-identical).
  CONCLUSION: pf.proj is the sole projection primitive on the seamed path; the
  swap is complete, there is no missed consumer.

## VERDICT: projection is a CONTRIBUTOR, NOT the culprit for the 22

The rounded PlaneFrame projection provably changes overlay decisions (486
crossings + 4 spurious merges on the dirty component) and moving to an EXACT
axis-drop projection closes 1 of 22 net while relocating the rest. The other 21
are PROJECTION-INVARIANT: exact-coordinate-selection input predicates leave them
open. This CONFIRMS f4-r4/r6/routeA/routeB at a new layer - the residue is
genuine near-tangent geometry (clusters of distinct triple points spread
1e-4..~2*eps), not a projection artifact. The one wall the projection swap does
NOT cross is exactly the one the campaign gates: an exact 2D arrangement over
CONSTRUCTED intersection points (orient2d on constructed points) that keeps a
near-tangent face's ~1e-8 crossings distinct AND consistent with its transversal
partner. Axis-drop makes the INPUT predicates exact; it does not make the
CONSTRUCTED-crossing arrangement exact, which is where the fans actually open.
