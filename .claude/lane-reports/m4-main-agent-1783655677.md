critical itself - the literal E' reading is unchanged there. Caps
stay per-critical (spec R3). Residuals to record honestly in doc:

full yz edge) gives that run's non-canonical cap macro area whose
corners no strip carries - non-manifold in HEAD TOO (dead zone,
candidate SubEpsFeature tightening); (b) multi-sliver runs (gap >
eps in total) weld strip-strip at > eps distance - also inherited
from HEAD's behavior, unchanged by this rule for 2-crit runs which
