## KEY STRUCTURAL OBSERVATION (audit 1 pre-read)
The GUARD and the SNAP iterate the SAME `verts` set. Guard admits iff
|dot(N, vertPos[v]-cen)| <= eps for EVERY v. Snap displacement of v =
dot(N, vertPos[v]-cen)*N, |displacement| = |dot(N, vertPos[v]-cen)|.
=> the guarded quantity IS the per-vertex displacement magnitude. The guard is
already PER-VERTEX (L-infinity over all cluster verts), not per-plane-residual.
So audit-1's adversarial "residual fine but a vertex moves > eps" is NOT
constructible against this code. To confirm: instrument max displacement.

## AUDIT 3: pairGap = MIN of directional maxes - CONFIRMED SOUND (smoking gun at eps/2)
di = max over j-verts of dist to i-plane; dj = max over i-verts of dist to
j-plane; pairGap = min(di,dj). Near-band top-cap pair (i=6,j=15):
  di=3.600e-12, dj=1.440e-12, min=1.440e-12, max=3.600e-12.
- At full eps=5.499e-12: min AND max both < eps (admitted either way).
- At eps/2=2.749e-12 (the TOL-INVARIANCE run): min=1.44e-12 < eps/2 (admit),
  but max=3.60e-12 > eps/2 (REJECT). So MAX would wrongly reject the genuine
  near pair at eps/2 -> the tol-invariance resolve would break -> a hole. MIN
  correctly admits. This is the "diameter-amplified red herring" made concrete.
- Directionally: min<=max always, so {min<eps} is a SUPERSET of {max<eps}; min
  admits MORE. Over-permissive clustering is BACKSTOPPED by the per-vertex guard
  (L-inf over the whole cluster fail-closes anything not within eps of one
  plane), so min can never cause a WRONG resolve, only more guard work. A macro
  transversal has BOTH di,dj large -> min large -> rejected (min never wrongly
  admits a transversal). MAX would cause FALSE REJECTIONS (holes / tol-variance).
  => MIN is the sound admission metric; MAX is unsound (rejects genuine cases).
