### Design (pre-code)
The perturbed 4x4 orient3d determinant is sum_K coeff_K * e^K; SoS returns the
sign of the lowest-K nonzero coeff. Perturbation keys keyOf(r,c)=2^(rank*3+(2-c))
are 12 distinct powers of two, so K is a bitmask of perturbed entries and each
coeff_K is a signed sum of products of <= 3 of the 12 coord mantissas (a minor).
=> ONE integer routine SumSign(terms) where each term is (192-bit |product of <=3
mantissas|, 2-exponent esum, sign): align all terms to emin, accumulate in an
ADAPTIVE-width two's-complement bigint (nLimbs computed from the term spread),
read the sign. No expansion arithmetic; no window-fail (nLimbs sized to the
worst-case double spread => provably total). e^0 (ExactOrient3D) = the K=0 group;
full SoS = all 192 monomials grouped by K ascending, first nonzero sign.
Worst case: esum in [-3378,2913] (E=ex-53, ex in [-1073,1024]) -> spread<=6291;
bits <= 159+6291+carry -> nLimbs<=103; kAccumLimbs=112 covers it (heap-free,
rare filter-0 path). VALIDATION GATE before delete: new int-native must be
BITWISE-identical to old expansion on e^0 AND SoS (random/coplanar/mixed/wild/
adversary); then BridgedCaps re-oracled + all pins green.
